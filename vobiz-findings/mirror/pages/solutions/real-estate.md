# Real Estate

> Qualify leads and automate site-visit scheduling at scale with Vobiz - IVR routing, outbound call campaigns, and CRM integration for property developers and brokers across 130+ countries.

## What is Real Estate?

**Voice in Real Estate**

Real estate in India is a relationship business - but one operating at increasing scale and speed. Developers launch projects that attract thousands of enquiries in a matter of days. Brokers manage dozens of active buyers simultaneously. Property management companies field maintenance requests from hundreds of tenants. In every case, voice is the primary communication channel, and the volume of calls far exceeds what any manual process can handle efficiently.

Vobiz gives real estate developers, brokers, property management companies, and proptech platforms the voice infrastructure to scale their communications without scaling their headcount proportionally.

## The Problem It Solves

**Lead Management and Qualification**

When a new project is launched, a developer's phone lines can receive thousands of calls in the first 48 hours. Without an IVR and automated routing, this volume results in missed calls, frustrated enquirers, and lost leads. With Vobiz, every incoming call is answered immediately by an IVR that qualifies the caller - asking for the type of property they are interested in, their budget range, and their location preference. Based on the responses, the call is routed to the sales team member responsible for that segment.

Callers who cannot be connected to an agent immediately - because all agents are busy - are offered a callback option. The callback request is logged in the CRM via the Vobiz webhook, and a sales agent calls back within a defined SLA using the outbound calling API.

AI voice agents are increasingly used for initial lead qualification at scale. The AI handles the first call - gathering the prospect's requirements, answering basic questions about the project, and scheduling a site visit - and hands off to a human agent only when the prospect is genuinely qualified and ready for a detailed conversation. This allows a small sales team to handle a much larger volume of initial enquiries.

## How Vobiz Powers Real Estate

**Site Visit Coordination**

Site visits are the critical conversion event in real estate sales. Vobiz's appointment reminder system is used by developers and brokers to confirm site visit appointments, provide directions using text-to-speech, and handle last-minute reschedule requests. Automated reminder calls 24 hours before and on the morning of the visit significantly reduce the site visit no-show rate.

For property managers, the Conference API enables three-way calls between tenants reporting maintenance issues, property managers, and maintenance vendors - all coordinated and logged automatically through the property management platform.

## Why Choose Vobiz?

**Investor and Client Relationship Management**

High-value real estate clients expect responsive, personalised communication. Developers use Vobiz to build dedicated communication lines for investors - numbers that route directly to their relationship manager, not to a general IVR. The relationship manager's number is configured as the Dial destination in the XML for calls coming from that client's number.

All client communications are recorded and linked to the client record in the CRM via webhook. Post-call follow-up is tracked through the CDR API. The result is a complete, auditable record of every communication between the developer and the investor, accessible to the relationship manager at any time.
