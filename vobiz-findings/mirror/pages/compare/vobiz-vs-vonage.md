### Vonage Overview

* Acquired by Ericsson; focus on 5G and Global Network Platform
* Massive product suite: Voice, Video, SMS, WhatsApp, Verify, AI Studio
* Strong CRM integrations (Salesforce, Zendesk)
* Enterprise-heavy; support can be expensive
* No INR billing or TRAI compliance

### Vobiz Overview

* Simple, programmable voice API first
* Lower overhead and focused feature set
* Native INR/GST billing for local entities
* Fast onboarding without sales friction
* Indian DID numbers and low-latency local routing

## Summary

Vonage (Ericsson) operates a massive global enterprise suite, but for Indian businesses this often comes at a steep price. Billing is exclusively in USD, which introduces exchange rate risk and prevents you from reclaiming the 18% GST. Vonage's support is also tiered, requiring significant monthly fees to reach a human when you need integration help. Vobiz offers a streamlined, India-native alternative - the same Tier-1 voice infrastructure on a pure INR Pay-As-You-Go model, with low-latency local routing and 24/7 support included at no extra cost.
