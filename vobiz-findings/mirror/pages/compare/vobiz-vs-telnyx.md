## Summary

While Telnyx is renowned for its global private fiber backbone, its limited local footprint in India creates significant hurdles for Indian developers. Telnyx operates purely in USD, making your communication costs subject to global market volatility and preventing you from reclaiming GST. More critically, Telnyx does not natively support the 140, 160, or 92 series numbers that Indian businesses require for professional identity and compliance. Vobiz fills this gap with an India-native infrastructure that is fully TRAI compliant. Vobiz also provides direct interconnections with Indian operators, bypassing the complexity of international carrier handoffs and ensuring Tier-1 delivery rates for every call.
