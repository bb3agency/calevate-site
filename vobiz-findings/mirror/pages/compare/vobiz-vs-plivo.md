* Billing in USD; no INR options for standard accounts

## Summary

While Plivo provides a compliant infrastructure for the Indian market, the primary friction for growing developers is the Enterprise minimum spend. Plivo gates its highest concurrency and developer tools behind a \$1,000 USD monthly barrier, creating significant overhead for Indian startups. Vobiz removes this roadblock entirely by offering enterprise-grade concurrency and 140/160/92 series support on a pure Pay-As-You-Go model in INR. By billing in local currency and providing native GST invoices, Vobiz keeps your communication stack an asset rather than a complex accounting liability, letting you scale without the Enterprise premium.
