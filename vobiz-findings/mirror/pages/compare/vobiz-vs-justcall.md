## Vobiz vs JustCall: Developer Tool vs Sales Application

JustCall is a ready-to-use phone app for sales reps. Vobiz is a programmable API for building your own voice solutions. Choose the right one for your goals.

## Overview Comparison

| Feature | Vobiz | JustCall |
| :- | :- | :- |
| **Type** | Developer API (CPaaS) | Business App (UCaaS) |
| **Pricing** | PAYG per minute | Per user/month (\$29+) |
| **Custom Flows** | Full Programmability | Limited Automations |
| **Currency** | INR / GST | USD Only |
| **Cost (10 Users/10k min)** | \~₹4,500 | \~₹24,170 |

### JustCall Overview

* Cloud phone system designed for sales and support teams
* SaaS per-user pricing model
* 100+ CRM integrations (HubSpot, Pipedrive)
* Built-in AI dialer and call coaching tools
* Not a programmable API platform for builders

### Vobiz Overview

* Programmable API for custom voice applications
* Usage-based billing (no per-user fees)
* Voice XML (TwiML compatible) for logic
* WebSocket access for real-time AI agents
* Compliant with Indian telecom (TRAI/DND) requirements

* You have high volumes of talk-time

## Summary

JustCall is a capable plug-and-play application for American sales teams, but for Indian companies looking to scale, its per-user pricing model quickly becomes a significant seat-based tax. Billing in USD means you pay not just for calls but also for software overhead and currency conversion. Vobiz offers a purely programmable infrastructure model. By moving your communication engine to Vobiz, you pay only for the minutes you use, in INR, with full GST tax benefits. This lets you scale from 10 to 1,000 agents without subscription bloat on your monthly burn, while ensuring Tier-1 delivery across the Indian telecom grid.
