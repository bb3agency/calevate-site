## Vobiz vs TeleCRM: Programmable Infrastructure vs Sales Software

TeleCRM is a great tool for telecallers. Vobiz is the engine that powers voice applications. Learn why your choice depends on whether you want to use or build.

### TeleCRM Overview

* Sales CRM built for Indian telecalling teams
* Focus on lead management and automated follow-ups
* Built-in dialer and call recording
* Lead capture from Facebook, IndiaMart, etc.
* No API for external developers to build on top

### Vobiz Overview

* High-performance telephony platform
* Engineered for scale and low-latency voice
* Standard-compliant Voice XML and SDKs
* WebSocket streaming for real-time AI agents
* Partner portal for white-labeling and reselling

## Summary

TeleCRM is a specialized tool for tracking leads, but it is not a communication provider. At ₹849 per user, you are paying for the software UI while still lacking the native infrastructure to make professional business calls or provision official Indian phone numbers. Vobiz fills this gap by providing the full Tier-1 communication engine - API, SIP trunks, and 140/92 series DIDs - so you can build a professional calling experience without the per-seat tax. Vobiz lets you bypass the limitations of software-only CRMs and build a scalable, carrier-grade sales operation that you own and control.
