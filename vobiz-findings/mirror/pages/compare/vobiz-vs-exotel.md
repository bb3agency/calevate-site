## Summary

Exotel is a mature player in the Indian enterprise space, but for modern product teams, its sales-led friction is a significant barrier. While Exotel is fully compliant and provides Indian DIDs, getting started often requires multiple demos and custom quotes that take days. Vobiz provides a developer-first, instant-onboarding experience with transparent, published INR rates. Legacy providers often lock you into proprietary stacks (Ameyo/Harmony), whereas Vobiz works in an open integration environment. Whether you are using VAPI, Retell, or a custom stack, Vobiz provides the underlying carrier reliability without vendor lock-in.
