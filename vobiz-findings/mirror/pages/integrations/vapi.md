# Vapi integration

> Vapi connects to Vobiz SIP trunking for AI voice agent calling - enable outbound and inbound calls in 130+ countries via dashboard or API.

Vapi is a platform for building voice AI agents. Integrate with Vobiz to enable automated outbound and inbound calling through Vobiz SIP trunking, with your assistant reachable in 130+ countries.
