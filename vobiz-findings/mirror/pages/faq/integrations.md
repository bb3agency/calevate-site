# What integrations do you support?

> Vobiz natively supports LiveKit, Pipecat, Retell AI, ElevenLabs, VAPI, and SIP PBX systems - connect any AI voice agent or WebRTC stack to the global telephony network.

Vobiz is built ground-up for AI-first interconnectivity. Rather than treating AI systems as standard phones, it offers deep layer-7 protocols that bridge SIP telephony to modern AI WebRTC architectures with minimal overhead.

## Any SIP Provider

If your application does not rely heavily on AI, Vobiz also supports native **SIP Interoperability** out of the box. You can connect FreeSWITCH, Asterisk, 3CX, Cisco CallManager, or a legacy PBX via standard SIP trunking using either IP-ACL or credentials-based authentication.
