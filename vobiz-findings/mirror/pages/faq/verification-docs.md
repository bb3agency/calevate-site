# What documents are required for verification?

> Complete Vobiz KYC verification with GST certificate for organizations or Aadhaar and PAN for individuals - required before purchasing live numbers in India.

To comply with telecom regulations and prevent fraud or spoofing, all developers and organizations must complete standard KYC (Know Your Customer) verification before purchasing live numbers or launching outbound campaigns to public carrier networks.

## For Organizations & Companies

<Tip>
  When registering as a business entity, you must provide:

  * A valid Goods and Services Tax (GST) Number certificate.
  * In some carrier routing cases, an authorized signatory declaration.
</Tip>

## For Individuals & Sole Proprietors

<Info>
  When testing as an individual developer or sole proprietor, you must provide:

  * Government-issued Aadhaar Card
  * Permanent Account Number (PAN) Card
</Info>

<Note>
  **Data Privacy Notice:** All submitted verification documents are encrypted at rest and shared only with upstream telecom regulators for line activation compliance. Vobiz strictly adheres to applicable data-protection regulations and does not use these documents for any secondary or unauthorized purposes.
</Note>
