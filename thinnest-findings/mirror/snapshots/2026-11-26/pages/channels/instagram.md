# Instagram

> Your agent answers Instagram DMs and comments, and every conversation lands in your Inbox.

Connect an Instagram professional account and your agent answers its Direct
messages with the same knowledge and instructions it uses everywhere else. Every
conversation appears in the shared **Inbox**, where your team can read along and
reply.

You sign in with Meta's own login, using Facebook or Instagram. Nobody shares
a password, no Facebook Page is needed, and you can remove access at any time
from your Instagram or Meta business settings.

## Connect

**1. Open Connect Instagram**

In the sidebar, open **Instagram** and then **Connect Instagram**.

**2. Choose the agent that answers**

Pick the agent, then press **Connect with Instagram**.

**3. Sign in and choose your account**

Log in with Facebook or Instagram and choose the professional account to
connect. It must be a **Business** or **Creator** account.

**4. Send it a DM**

From another Instagram account, message yours. The agent replies within
seconds, and the conversation shows in the Inbox.

Each Instagram account is answered by one agent. To hand an account to a
different agent, change it on **Connect Instagram**. New conversations go to the
new agent, and open ones keep the agent they started with.

## What the agent handles

* **Text and links.** A long answer is split into several messages at a
  paragraph or sentence, because Instagram refuses a message over 1,000
  characters.
* **Photos customers send**, such as a reference image or a screenshot. They are
  kept with the conversation, so they are still there weeks later even though
  Instagram's own links expire.
* **Photos the agent sends.**
* Buttons and lists of choices are sent as plain text.

## Replying yourself

Open the conversation in the **Inbox** and reply. Your message goes out on
Instagram, and the agent stops answering that conversation while a person has
it.

You can also reply from the Instagram app on the business account. Your reply
shows in the Inbox as a message from your team, and the agent stops answering
that conversation, just as if you had replied from the Inbox.

> [!NOTE]
>
> Instagram lets a business reply for **24 hours** after the customer's last
> message, and a person up to **7 days**. After that, a reply is saved in the
> Inbox but not sent until the customer writes again. There are no templates on
> Instagram for reaching someone after the window closes.

## Ice breakers

Under **Instagram > Ice breakers**, set up to four questions people can tap when
they open a new chat with you, such as "What are your prices?" or "How do I
book?". They appear in Instagram as soon as you save, and the agent answers
whichever one a person taps.

## Comment automation

Under **Instagram > Comment automation**, add keyword rules for comments on your
posts. When a comment contains a keyword, the commenter gets a **private DM**,
and you can also post a **public reply** under the comment.

* Leave the private DM empty and your agent writes the answer itself.
* Rules are checked top to bottom, and the first match wins.
* Keywords match whole words, ignoring capitals and accents, so "price" matches
  "What's the PRICE?" but not "priceless".
* **Every other comment** decides what happens to comments no rule matches: the
  agent answers them by DM, or they are left alone.

Instagram allows **one** private DM per comment, within 7 days of it. When the
commenter replies to that DM, the conversation continues in the Inbox like any
other.

## IG Dashboard

**Instagram > IG Dashboard** shows the last 30 days for all your accounts or one
of them:

* DMs received, and from how many people
* the agent's replies
* your team's replies
* comments answered
* conversations a person has taken over
* replies Instagram refused

## Opting out

A customer who writes STOP is recorded as opted out, exactly as on WhatsApp,
and the agent stops replying to them.

## What it does not do

> [!WARNING]
>
> **No broadcasts or campaigns on Instagram.** Instagram does not allow a
> business to message people who have not written first, so Broadcasts,
> Sequences and Send Message work on WhatsApp only.
>
> **Instagram does not join a customer's identity.** Instagram gives a name or
> handle, never a phone number, so an Instagram conversation is not merged with
> the same person's WhatsApp or phone history.
>
> **Voice notes, videos and shared posts** are noted in the conversation, but
> the agent does not watch or listen to them.
