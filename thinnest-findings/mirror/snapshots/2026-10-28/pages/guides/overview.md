## In what order?

If you are new, this sequence wastes the least time:

**1. Get it answering**

[Quickstart](https://docs.thinnest.ai/quickstart.md), then run [the gaps
loop](https://docs.thinnest.ai/guides/reduce-i-dont-know.md) once. You will learn more about your own
customers in half an hour than in a week of planning.

**2. Make it useful**

[Order status](https://docs.thinnest.ai/guides/order-status.md) — the point where an agent stops being
a search box with manners.

**3. Make it personal**

[Signed-in customers](https://docs.thinnest.ai/guides/nextjs-identity.md). Everything downstream gets
better once the agent knows who it is talking to.

**4. Then reach out**

[Broadcasts](https://docs.thinnest.ai/guides/first-campaign.md) and
[sequences](https://docs.thinnest.ai/guides/abandoned-cart.md) last. Outbound to people your agent
cannot yet help well is a way to spend money on disappointing customers.
