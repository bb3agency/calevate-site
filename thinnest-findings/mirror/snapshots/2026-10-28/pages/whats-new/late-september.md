# Late September 2026

> What changed in ThinnestAI from September 25 to 30, 2026.

These are earlier changes. The newest are on [What's New](https://docs.thinnest.ai/whats-new.md), and the
days just after this are on [Early October](https://docs.thinnest.ai/whats-new/early-october.md).

**Sep 30, 2026**

Products in the chat, email notifications, a Pro plan

**New**

* **Show your products in the WhatsApp chat.** The agent can send a product from
  your own catalogue, a few of them, or none. You link the catalogue in Meta's
  Commerce Manager first.
  [Products](https://docs.thinnest.ai/whatsapp/products.md)
* **Be told by email, from your own Resend account, about what you choose.**
  [Notifications](https://docs.thinnest.ai/workspace/notifications.md#receive-notifications-to-email)
* **A Pro plan.** It sits between pay-as-you-go and Scale, with a monthly usage
  credit that expires, Studio voices and cloned voices.
  [Voice](https://docs.thinnest.ai/channels/voice.md#voice)

**Improved**

* **Saved replies are added and edited from the inbox,** and the composer's
  format, emoji and link buttons work. [Saved replies](https://docs.thinnest.ai/workspace/inbox.md#saved-replies)

**Sep 29, 2026**

Studio voices and Voice Clone

**New**

* **A third voice tier, Studio.** Your own cloned voices plus a large library of
  more, with one voice free to preview on every plan. Studio also works through
  the API. [Voice](https://docs.thinnest.ai/channels/voice.md#voice)
* **Voice Clone has its own page.** Record a sample, with consent, and your
  agent speaks in it.
  [Voice clone](https://docs.thinnest.ai/channels/voice-clone.md)
* **Add a contact by hand.** [Contacts](https://docs.thinnest.ai/agent/leads.md#contacts)

**Improved**

* **Your business name and the blue badge are explained as the two separate
  approvals they are.**
  [Your name, and the badge](https://docs.thinnest.ai/channels/whatsapp#your-name-and-the-badge-are-two-different-approvals)

**Sep 28, 2026**

Send a template when the 24-hour window has closed

**New**

* **Send a template from a conversation whose window has closed.** A **Send a
  template** button replaces the reply box, and you fill in every blank yourself,
  so nobody is greeted with a template's example values.
  [Whether you can reply freely](https://docs.thinnest.ai/workspace/inbox.md#whether-you-can-reply-freely)

**Sep 27, 2026**

See the 24-hour window on the conversation

**New**

* **The 24-hour window is shown above the reply box.** A line counts down what is
  left, turns amber in the last hour, and is replaced by a note when the window
  runs out. A customer who has never written has no window, and the thread says
  so. [Whether you can reply freely](https://docs.thinnest.ai/workspace/inbox.md#whether-you-can-reply-freely)

**Improved**

* **A person you imported is one contact when they message,** not two.
  [Contacts](https://docs.thinnest.ai/agent/leads.md#contacts)

**Sep 26, 2026**

Consent, delivery and who bills you

**New**

* **Record consent for people already in Contacts** with a bulk button, as well
  as at import, in the conversation and through the API.
  [Consent](https://docs.thinnest.ai/agent/leads.md#consent)

**Improved**

* **A message that was not delivered says so,** with WhatsApp's own reason, and
  is not charged for. [When a message isn't delivered](https://docs.thinnest.ai/whatsapp/not-delivered.md)
* **Edit your WhatsApp business profile on Connect WhatsApp:** picture, tagline,
  description, address and category.
  [Connecting](https://docs.thinnest.ai/channels/whatsapp)

**Sep 25, 2026**

Leads and Contacts split, a clearer fee, campaigns on a low balance

**New**

* **Leads and Contacts are two pages,** split by who did the reaching.
  [Contacts](https://docs.thinnest.ai/agent/leads.md#contacts)
* **A number tells you when Meta has stopped it,** and why. Most often it is a
  failed payment method on the Meta account.
  [When WhatsApp stops the number sending](https://docs.thinnest.ai/channels/whatsapp#when-whatsapp-stops-the-number-sending)

**Improved**

* **The fee is named: a 10% ThinnestAI platform fee** on every message sent on
  your own WhatsApp account, shown beside Meta's own charge.
  [What gets charged](https://docs.thinnest.ai/meta/windows-and-pricing.md#who-bills-you-and-for-what)
* **A voice campaign on a low balance stops redialling first,** and the last of
  your balance goes to people it has never tried.
  [When your balance runs low](https://docs.thinnest.ai/channels/voice-campaigns.md#when-your-balance-runs-low)
