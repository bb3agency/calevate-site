# Business verification

> What it unlocks, what it costs you in time, and what stays locked without it.

Meta verifies that your business is a real, registered entity. Several things
you will want are gated behind it, and the wait is measured in days rather than
minutes — so start it early.

## What it unlocks

| | Without verification | With |
| - | - | - |
| Messaging limit | 250 unique customers / 24h | **2,000**, then automatic increases |
| One-time password templates | **Blocked** | Available |
| Publishing a form | **Blocked** — it builds and previews, but reaches nobody | Available |
| Display name on your number | Limited | Your verified business name |

<Warning>
  **Forms are the one that surprises people.** You can build a form, store it and
  preview it perfectly. Meta will not *publish* a flow for an unverified
  business, and an unpublished form reaches no customer — so the feature looks
  finished and does nothing.
</Warning>

## What Meta asks for

Broadly: a legal business name that matches official documents, a business
address, a phone number or website that can be checked, and a document proving
the entity exists — in India typically a GST certificate, Certificate of
Incorporation, or a utility bill in the business name.

The single most common cause of rejection is a **mismatch**: the name on your
Meta business portfolio differing, even slightly, from the name on the document.
Match them exactly, including the `Pvt Ltd` or `LLP` suffix.

## How long

Days, occasionally longer. Meta may ask for more documents, which restarts the
clock.

<Tip>
  Start verification on day one, before you need any of what it unlocks. It runs
  in the background while you build everything else, and nothing about it gets
  faster by waiting.
</Tip>

## Connecting your number

**Connect WhatsApp**, in the sidebar, is self-serve — it opens Meta's own signup,
you sign in with your own Facebook account, and it never touches us. See
[Connecting](/channels/whatsapp#connecting) for the two ways in and which
number to use.
