# Where you are billed

> One app for every country: what changes with the country your business is billed in.

ThinnestAI is one app, at `app.thinnest.ai`, for every country. When you
create a workspace it asks which country your business is billed in. That
answer sets what you pay in and a few things you can use.

| | India | Everywhere else |
| - | - | - |
| You pay in | Rupees (₹), with a GST invoice | US dollars (\$) |
| Phone numbers | Buy an Indian number from us, or bring your own | Buy a United States number from us, or bring your own |
| Phone calls are charged | In half-minute steps | In whole minutes, at the same rate a minute |
| API address | `app.thinnest.ai/api/v1` | `app.thinnest.ai/api/v1` |

## Choosing

The sign-up page starts the country on where your network says you are. Change
it if your business is billed somewhere else. It is set when the workspace is
created, and a workspace stays in the market it was created in.

> [!NOTE]
>
> An agent, a wallet or a balance cannot be moved from one workspace to
> another. If you need a workspace in the other market, create a new one and
> set it up again.

A few countries we cannot take payments from yet. If yours is one of them, the
sign-up page says so before it creates anything.

## What you pay in

A workspace billed outside India is billed in **US dollars**. Prices, your
wallet, invoices and the WhatsApp message rates on the [Usage](https://docs.thinnest.ai/workspace/usage.md)
page all appear in dollars, never converted from rupees. A workspace billed in
India is billed in rupees only.

## Voices

A workspace billed outside India has one voice tier, **Studio**, with the whole
voice library. Ten British voices — five women and five men, chosen for
answering the phone for a business — come first, and new agents start on the
first of them. On the Playground, **Browse voices** opens the library: search
it, filter by language, accent, category, gender, age and tag, listen, and
select. Or choose through the API's [List Voices](https://docs.thinnest.ai/api-reference/voices/list-voices.md)
and the agent's `voice` setting. See [Voice](https://docs.thinnest.ai/channels/voice.md).

## Phone numbers

A workspace billed outside India can buy a **United States** local number from
us, or bring a number it already owns from any country and point it at the agent.
A United States number from us calls the United States and Canada only; see
[United States numbers](https://docs.thinnest.ai/channels/phone-numbers.md#united-states-numbers).
Whichever market you are in, give every number with its country code. See [Phone
numbers](https://docs.thinnest.ai/channels/phone-numbers.md#country-codes).

## Phone calls

A phone call on a workspace billed outside India is charged in **whole minutes**,
rounded up: a 20-second call is one minute, a 61-second call two. The rate a
minute is the same as anywhere else on your plan. A workspace billed in India is
charged in half-minute steps. Web calls are charged by the second in both.

## WhatsApp

Messages are priced from Meta's own published rates for the country they go to,
in your workspace's currency. You pay Meta directly for messages sent from your
own WhatsApp account; what we add is our platform fee. See [WhatsApp
windows and pricing](https://docs.thinnest.ai/meta/windows-and-pricing.md).

## Resellers

A [white-label](https://docs.thinnest.ai/white-label/overview.md) reseller billed outside India sets
prices in dollars and connects **its own Dodo Payments account** to be
paid by its clients. See [Your prices](https://docs.thinnest.ai/white-label/prices.md#paid-through-dodo-international).
