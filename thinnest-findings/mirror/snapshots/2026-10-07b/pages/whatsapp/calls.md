# WhatsApp calls

> Customers call your business from WhatsApp, and your agent answers.

A customer taps the call button in your WhatsApp chat, and your agent picks up —
the same agent, with the same voice, knowledge and tools, that answers your phone
line and your website. No phone number to rent, and nothing for the customer to
install: they are already in the chat.

## Turning it on

1. On **Connect WhatsApp**, press **Calls** on the number.
2. Switch on **WhatsApp calls** and save.

That is all. Switching it on also turns on **Answer calls** for the agent on that
number, and gives it a voice if it had none. Its **WhatsApp** row on the Voice page
shows **On** — it follows this switch, and every agent has it on by default.

<Note>
  The **WhatsApp calls** switch is the one that decides. While it is off, this
  number answers no calls — even if the same setting is on in Meta's WhatsApp
  Manager. Switching it off turns the agent's WhatsApp row off too, and leaves the
  website and phone as they are.
</Note>

## The settings

| Setting | What it does |
| - | - |
| **WhatsApp calls** | On or off for this number. |
| **Show the call button** | Whether customers see a call button in your chat. They can still call from a message that has a call button. |
| **Allow callback requests** | After a customer calls (answered or missed), WhatsApp asks them whether your business may call them back. Their answer appears in the inbox as a message — for example *Allowed you to call them back on WhatsApp, for the next 7 days*. Calling them back over WhatsApp from ThinnestAI is coming next. |
| **Call hours** | When customers can call. Outside them, WhatsApp offers to chat or request a callback instead. Off means always available. |
| **Closed days** | Up to 20 dates — holidays, events — when calls aren't taken. |

The settings are read from WhatsApp each time you open them, so a change made in
Meta's WhatsApp Manager shows up here too.

Pick each time from the list, or type it however you like — `09:00`, `9 am`, `18:00` and `6 pm` all work.
Switching call hours off really
turns them off at WhatsApp, even if you cleared the days first.

Call hours are edited one range a day here. WhatsApp allows two a day; a second
range set in WhatsApp Manager is kept as it is unless you edit that day.

**Customers don't see your hours listed anywhere** — not on your profile, not in
the chat. They meet them when they call outside them: WhatsApp offers to chat or
to request a callback, and shows when you're next available. WhatsApp applies a
change straight away, but a customer's phone can take **up to 7 days** to pick
it up; opening your business's chat info page on that phone refreshes it at once.
To test your hours, call from another phone at a time they're closed.

## Who can use it

WhatsApp only enables calling for businesses with a **daily messaging limit of at
least 2,000 people** — unique people you start conversations with in a rolling
24 hours, counted across your whole business, not per number. Every new business
starts lower. The quickest way up is [verifying your business](/meta/verification);
otherwise Meta raises it as you send messages people engage with
([how limits rise](/meta/quality-and-limits#climbing)).

The Calls window states your business's limit. If it is under 2,000, it says so
over its settings and holds the Save button until it is.

<Note>
  WhatsApp Manager may show an older **per-number** limit, often 250. That figure
  does not decide calling — the business-wide one does, and it is often already
  2,000.
</Note>

While **WhatsApp calls** is off, the other settings in the window are greyed out.

## What it costs

A WhatsApp call is billed at your **voice per-minute rate** — the same as a phone
call — for the time your agent is on it, rounded up to the next whole minute.
Calls your customers make to you cost nothing from Meta.

## Not here yet

* **Your agent calling customers on WhatsApp.** Coming next: callbacks, "ring the
  customer now", voice campaigns and the call API will be able to use WhatsApp, for
  customers who have allowed calls from your business.
* Video calls.

If a call doesn't connect, the reason is shown in WhatsApp's words with what it
means — see [WhatsApp error codes](https://thinnest.ai/whatsapp-error-codes).
