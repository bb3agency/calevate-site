# Clone Your Voice

> Clone your voice with Bolna AI. Upload a short sample to create lifelike custom voices and personalize your AI agents with any licensed voice.

## What is Voice Cloning?

Create custom AI voices by uploading a short audio sample (1-2 minutes) of any voice you have rights to use. Perfect for brand consistency, personalized customer experiences, or using specific voice talent across all your agents.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Brand Consistency" icon="building">

  </Card>

  <Card title="Personalization" icon="user">

## How to Clone a Voice

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Audio Tab">
    Go to your agent in the Bolna Playground and click the **Audio** tab. Scroll down to the **Text-to-Speech** section and click **Add Voice +**.

    <Frame caption="Text-to-Speech section in the Audio Tab with Add Voice button">
      <img src="https://mintcdn.com/bolna-54a2d4fe/mJ1zPF3eB4Dyupzj/images/clone_voices_1.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=mJ1zPF3eB4Dyupzj&q=85&s=b0886657f41e04c9832e9afc7e62d445" alt="Audio Tab showing the Text-to-Speech section with Add Voice button" width="789" height="349" data-path="images/clone_voices_1.png" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select Provider">
    Choose your voice cloning provider: **ElevenLabs** or **Cartesia**

    <Frame caption="Select your voice provider">
      <img src="https://mintcdn.com/bolna-54a2d4fe/wGMsKRPKze41oXTD/images/clone_voices_2.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=wGMsKRPKze41oXTD&q=85&s=b3e3172da6c9f03c680e3984a69e0cb4" alt="Voice cloning provider selection screen" width="836" height="720" data-path="images/clone_voices_2.png" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Name Your Voice">
    Enter a **name** and **description** to identify your voice when building agents

    <Frame caption="Enter a name for your cloned voice">
      <img src="https://mintcdn.com/bolna-54a2d4fe/mJ1zPF3eB4Dyupzj/images/clone_voices_3.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=mJ1zPF3eB4Dyupzj&q=85&s=a6ce2231bb0786c2df1ae629370e4353" alt="Voice naming interface" width="696" height="593" data-path="images/clone_voices_3.png" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Upload Audio Sample">
    Upload a high-quality audio file (MP3, WAV) that is **1-2 minutes** long

    <Frame caption="Upload a 1-2 minute audio sample">
      <img src="https://mintcdn.com/bolna-54a2d4fe/mJ1zPF3eB4Dyupzj/images/clone_voices_4.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=mJ1zPF3eB4Dyupzj&q=85&s=0b825658a8500108d07125c1c2bca935" alt="Audio sample upload interface" width="687" height="586" data-path="images/clone_voices_4.png" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Clone Voice">
    Click **"Clone Voice"** and wait for processing. Your new voice will appear in Voice Lab!
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  For best results, use clean audio with **no background noise** and only the voice you want to clone.
</Tip>
