# Link Your Plivo Account to Bolna for Voice AI

> Securely connect your Plivo account with Bolna. Enable your Voice AI agents to utilize Plivo phone numbers for managing inbound and outbound calls.

## Use your own Plivo account to make outbound calls

You can connect your own Plivo account and start using it on Bolna. All calls initiated from Bolna will be from your own Plivo account and use your own Plivo phone numbers.

1. Navigate to `Providers` tab from the left menu bar & Click **Plivo connect button**.

</Frame>

<br />

2. Fill in the required details.

<Frame caption="Provide Plivo details">
  <img src="https://mintcdn.com/bolna-54a2d4fe/mJ1zPF3eB4Dyupzj/images/connect_plivo_account_step_2.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=mJ1zPF3eB4Dyupzj&q=85&s=8edd9d04b550c29a566cd0daa6484278" alt="Plivo account connection form in Bolna showing Auth ID and Auth Token input fields for establishing secure connection" width="1766" height="1294" data-path="images/connect_plivo_account_step_2.png" />
</Frame>

<br />

3. Save details by clicking on the **connect button**.

<Frame caption="Connect your account">
  <img src="https://mintcdn.com/bolna-54a2d4fe/mJ1zPF3eB4Dyupzj/images/connect_plivo_account_step_3.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=mJ1zPF3eB4Dyupzj&q=85&s=1ea1cceff1bf28009b1d43ff8a88d4ce" alt="Plivo account connection confirmation screen in Bolna showing connect button to finalize telephony provider integration" width="1996" height="1438" data-path="images/connect_plivo_account_step_3.png" />
</Frame>

<br />

4. You'll see that your Plivo account was successfully connected. All your calls will now go via your own Plivo account and phone numbers.
