# Securely Link Your Twilio Account to Bolna

> Follow detailed steps to connect your Twilio account with Bolna. Enable the use of your Twilio phone numbers for both inbound and outbound Voice AI calls.

## Use your own Twilio account to make outbound calls

<Note>
  We connect your `Twilio` account securely via using [infisical](https://infisical.com/).
</Note>

You can connect your own Twilio account and start using it on Bolna. All calls initiated from Bolna will be from your own Twilio account and use your own Twilio phone numbers.

1. Navigate to `Providers` tab from the left menu bar & Click **Twilio connect button**.

</Frame>

<br />

2. Fill in the required details.

<Frame caption="Provide Twilio details">
  <img src="https://mintcdn.com/bolna-54a2d4fe/mJ1zPF3eB4Dyupzj/images/connect_twilio_account_step_2.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=mJ1zPF3eB4Dyupzj&q=85&s=a798020a17d9dc91d6d666a3b2bb387d" alt="Twilio account connection form in Bolna showing Account SID and Auth Token input fields for secure Twilio connection" width="1776" height="1332" data-path="images/connect_twilio_account_step_2.png" />
</Frame>

<br />

3. Save details by clicking on the **connect button**.

<Frame caption="Connect your account">
  <img src="https://mintcdn.com/bolna-54a2d4fe/mJ1zPF3eB4Dyupzj/images/connect_twilio_account_step_3.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=mJ1zPF3eB4Dyupzj&q=85&s=1b426c8ac641a526dc04ca091a91ab9b" alt="Twilio account connection confirmation screen in Bolna showing connect button to finalize Twilio telephony connection" width="2064" height="1642" data-path="images/connect_twilio_account_step_3.png" />
</Frame>

<br />

4. You'll see that your Twilio account was successfully connected. All your calls will now go via your own Twilio account and phone numbers.
