# Link Your Vobiz Account to Bolna for Voice AI

> Securely connect your Vobiz account with Bolna. Enable your Voice AI agents to utilize Vobiz phone numbers for managing inbound and outbound calls.

## Use your own Vobiz account to make outbound calls

You can connect your own Vobiz account and start using it on Bolna. All calls initiated from Bolna will be from your own Vobiz account and use your own Vobiz phone numbers.

1. Navigate to `Providers` tab from the left menu bar & Click **Vobiz connect button**.

2. Fill in the required details.

<Frame caption="Provide Vobiz details">
  <img src="https://mintcdn.com/bolna-54a2d4fe/yxHicYPn3ibZ43_e/images/connect_vobiz_account_step_2.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=yxHicYPn3ibZ43_e&q=85&s=732a64ed5686eb8b2c66c63f517bb00c" alt="Vobiz account connection form in Bolna showing Auth ID and Auth Token input fields for establishing secure connection" width="2062" height="1330" data-path="images/connect_vobiz_account_step_2.png" />

<br />

3. Save details by clicking on the **connect button**.

<Frame caption="Connect your account">
  <img src="https://mintcdn.com/bolna-54a2d4fe/yxHicYPn3ibZ43_e/images/connect_vobiz_account_step_3.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=yxHicYPn3ibZ43_e&q=85&s=14306a15337927c377d74e6b745457a0" alt="Vobiz account connection confirmation screen in Bolna showing connect button to finalize telephony provider integration" width="1139" height="570" data-path="images/connect_vobiz_account_step_3.png" />

<br />

4. You'll see that your Vobiz account was successfully connected. All your calls will now go via your own Vobiz account and phone numbers.
