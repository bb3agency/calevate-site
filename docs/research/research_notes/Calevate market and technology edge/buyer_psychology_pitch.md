# Buyer psychology and pitch: how Indian (Telugu-first) SMB owners perceive and buy AI calling, Oct 2026

All sources read on 2026-10-30. Evidence labels used throughout:
- **SURVEY**: a survey with a stated method (third-party or vendor-run).
- **VENDOR-CLAIM**: a vendor's own marketing or case-study figure. Not independently verified.
- **ANECDOTE/OPINION**: one person's account (founder post, blog opinion).
- **UNKNOWN**: searched for and not found.

Scope caveat: this pass used 15 search/fetch calls. Reddit, YouTube comments, Google/Play Store reviews and LinkedIn threads did **not** surface through web search: no subreddit thread on r/india, r/IndiaBusiness or r/developersIndia about AI telecallers was returned by three differently phrased queries. Owner-voice quotes are therefore thin. The strongest owner-side material is one founder's first-hand account on X. Everything else is surveys of the general Indian public or vendor content.

## 1. What Indian SMB owners fear or doubt about AI calling

### Takeaway
The only ranked survey data on Indian SMB AI concerns (Salesforce) puts security first, then a tie between compliance and customer distrust of AI, then data quality. Fears specific to voice (robotic voice, Telugu accuracy, cost surprises) appear mainly in vendor content that pre-empts them, plus one founder's account. No primary owner-voice source specific to Telugu was found.

### Cited Findings
- SURVEY (Salesforce SMB Trends; global sample of 3,350 SMB leaders in 26 countries, Aug 3 to Sep 16, 2024; India subset size not stated). The top three AI concerns of Indian SMBs are (1) security risks, (2) a tie between compliance concerns and customer distrust of AI, and (3) poor data quality. No percentages are published for the tie. — [CRN India](https://www.crn.in/?p=68989); [CXOToday](https://cxotoday.com/media-coverage/salesforce-report-93-of-indian-smbs-using-ai-grew-their-revenue/); [Entrepreneur India](https://india.entrepreneur.com/news-and-trends/93-of-indian-smbs-using-ai-reported-increase-in-revenue/484534)
- SURVEY (same Salesforce release): 78% of Indian SMBs use or experiment with AI, and 93% of the Indian SMBs using AI report that it raised revenue. Both are self-reported. — [IndiaAI](https://indiaai.gov.in/article/78-of-indian-smbs-leverage-ai-for-business-success-salesforce-report); [CXOToday](https://cxotoday.com/media-coverage/salesforce-report-93-of-indian-smbs-using-ai-grew-their-revenue/)
- ANECDOTE (Yash Vijayvargiya, Skill Arbitrage, post on X, describing a test that began March 2025; Hindi outbound to about 30,000 leads a month). His points:
  - He names the common belief: "Indians will not talk to robots" (his words).
  - His team expected 30–40% immediate hang-ups and saw 6%.
  - The first batch failed because the voice was "too perfect". Conversion was worse than their worst human caller.
  - Adding slight background noise and pauses raised conversion by 40%.
  - He went through three vendors. He says the vendor's tech is cheap (₹1–5/min) and that the real work is process design. — [X post](https://x.com/Yash912/status/2041917124046991780?lang=en)
- VENDOR-CLAIM / OPINION. Vendor content pre-empts accuracy doubts, which shows they are live objections:
  - "most platforms claim 'Hindi support'", but few ship production-grade Hindi on real telephony audio. — [caller.digital](https://caller.digital/blog/best-hindi-voice-ai-agent-platform-india-2026)
  - Accuracy degrades with heavy regional accents on noisy lines, and error rates on regional languages over phone audio can be several times US-English error rates. — [caller.digital buyer's guide](https://caller.digital/blog/voice-ai-india-2026-complete-guide); [Analytics Insight (ConvoZen)](https://analyticsinsight.net/artificial-intelligence/why-indian-businesses-need-voice-ai-that-understands-code-switching-how-convozen-addresses-mixed-language-customer-calls)
- VENDOR-CLAIM (JoyzAI, Telugu buyer's guide, 28 Sep 2026). It tells Telugu buyers to test:
  - regional variation;
  - local place, product and number names;
  - Telugu–English code-switching;
  - noise;
  - interruption handling, meaning whether the agent stops and keeps context. — [JoyzAI](https://joyz.ai/blog/best-ai-voice-calling-agent-in-telugu/)
- VENDOR-CLAIM (caller.digital). On cost surprises, it says effective cost runs 2–4× the headline per-minute rate once these are added:
  - platform fees of ₹15k–2L/month;
  - minimum commitments of ₹50k–5L;
  - telephony markup of 2–5×;
  - regional-language surcharges of ₹1–3/min;
  - onboarding fees of ₹1–10L. — [caller.digital pricing](https://caller.digital/voice-ai-pricing-india)
- VENDOR-CLAIM (MyOperator, analysis of 200+ live agents, updated 24 Oct 2026). On staff job loss, it frames AI as "not about replacing humans": the three case studies added zero headcount and moved staff to judgement work. It runs no survey of owner or staff fears. — [MyOperator](https://myoperator.com/blog/how-indian-smbs-use-ai-agents-2026-myoperator-deployments)
- VENDOR-CLAIM (Outpero, 14 Oct 2026). Positions a hybrid model: AI for repetitive, time-critical calls and humans for novel, negotiation or complaint calls. — [Outpero cost blog](https://www.outpero.com/blog/telugu-ai-voice-agent-vs-human-telecaller-cost)
- VENDOR-CLAIM (caller.digital FAQ). Calls the objection "Our customers won't accept AI" outdated in 2026. This is a sales claim. — [caller.digital FAQ](https://caller.digital/blog/voice-ai-india-faq-50-questions-2026)

### Inferences
- The two fears with evidence behind them are customer distrust of AI (Salesforce tie, the founder's hang-up expectation) and security/compliance (Salesforce #1 and #2). "Telugu accent quality" and "cost surprises" are evidenced only as objections vendors pre-empt. That makes them probable, not measured.
- The founder's lesson that sounding human converts is double-edged for Calevate. His fix moved toward making the call feel like a person, while Calevate discloses the AI by design. His more transferable point is that relevance and respect for time drove engagement, not whether the voice was artificial.

### Gaps
- UNKNOWN: any Telugu-speaking SMB owner's own words (Reddit, YouTube, Google reviews) on AI calling. Web search returned none, and a direct Reddit or YouTube crawl was not possible in this pass.
- UNKNOWN: a survey quantifying owner fear of staff job loss, or of customers learning the caller is AI.
- UNKNOWN: Telangana versus Andhra dialect expectations. No source distinguishes them.

## 2. How customers in India react to AI voice calls, including disclosure and regional languages

### Takeaway
Indians are both excited (65%) and nervous (66%) about AI, and 67% expect AI use to be disclosed (Ipsos 2025). Their reference point for automated calls is a flood of spam: 4,168 crore spam calls in 2025 per Truecaller, and about 90% get pesky calls despite DND per LocalCircles. India-specific hang-up or disclosure-effect data for AI voice calls was not found.

### Cited Findings
- SURVEY (Ipsos AI Monitor 2025, 30 countries, about 23,000 respondents; released 19 Jun 2025; India sample size not stated). For India:
  - 67% expect AI products and services to disclose AI use;
  - 65% are excited about AI and 66% are nervous about it;
  - 65% trust the government to regulate AI responsibly;
  - 55% expect a positive impact on jobs. — [Ipsos India](https://www.ipsos.com/en-in/trust-and-transparency-emerge-key-cornerstones-ai-usage-among-indians-ipsos-ai-monitor-2025)
- SURVEY (Zendesk CX Trends 2025, as reported by IndiaAI). 81% of Indian consumers would engage more with AI if it felt more human-like, and 63% are more likely to trust AI with friendly, empathetic traits. — [IndiaAI](https://indiaai.gov.in/article/81-of-indian-consumers-seek-more-human-like-interactions-zendesk-2025-cx-trends-report)
- SURVEY (ServiceNow, Mar 2025). 80% of Indian consumers use AI chatbots for tasks such as complaint status. Chat only, not voice. — [ServiceNow PDF](https://s205.q4cdn.com/537566246/files/doc_news/AI-to-bridge-Indias-customer-service-gap-as-80-of-consumers-rely-on-chatbots-for-key-services-03-24-2025-traffic-2025.pdf)
- SURVEY, global and not India. Avaya reports that 87% of consumers want businesses to disclose AI use and 90% want access to a human. — [Avaya](https://www.avaya.com/en/blogs/customer-experience-statistics-2026/)
- VENDOR SURVEY, US only. 22% of US consumers would hang up if they learned mid-call that they were talking to AI. — [AnsweringServiceCare](https://answeringservicecare.com/insights/robots-reveal-yourself-the-ai-call-report/)
- DATA (Truecaller 2025 India report): 4,168 crore spam calls received in 2025, 1,189 crore of them blocked. Truecaller warns AI-generated voice scams could rise in 2026. — [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/india/story/indians-received-over-4000-crore-spam-calls-in-2025-1189-crore-blocked-report-515765-2026-02-11); [FoneArena](https://www.fonearena.com/blog/475283/india-received-4168-crore-spam-calls-2025-truecaller.html)
- SURVEY (LocalCircles, as reported):
  - About 90% get pesky calls despite DND registration (date not shown in the result). — [BOOM](https://www.boomlive.in/web-stories/90-of-indians-receive-spam-calls-despite-being-in-the-dnd-list-study)
  - A survey of 14,000+ subscribers in 309 districts found 27% saw fewer pesky calls, and experts flagged a shift toward robocalls (about Nov 2024). — [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/27-mobile-subscribers-claims-reduction-in-pesky-calls-says-survey-3290056); [Business Today](https://businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/trais-crackdown-on-spam-calls-shows-mixed-results-robo-calls-on-the-rise-454944-2024-11-25)
- ANECDOTE: a 6% immediate hang-up rate on Hindi AI outbound to warm leads (see §1). — [X post](https://x.com/Yash912/status/2041917124046991780?lang=en)

### Inferences
- Outbound AI calls in India land in a spam-saturated, low-trust environment. Inbound (the customer called the business) and warm callbacks (the customer just enquired) are the contexts where the "robot" objection is weakest. That fits Calevate's no-cold-calls stance.
- Ipsos's 67% "expect disclosure" supports Calevate's mandatory disclosure as a consumer expectation, not just a legal cost. US data suggests the damage comes from discovering the AI mid-call, which argues for disclosing early. That is a cross-market inference, not India data.

### Gaps
- UNKNOWN: any India or Telugu measurement of hang-up rate with versus without AI disclosure.
- UNKNOWN: regional-language-specific acceptance data. The claim that tier-2/3 customers prefer regional-language calls appears only in vendor FAQs without a cited survey.

## 3. Messaging frames competitors and adjacent SaaS use

### Takeaway
Telugu AI-calling competitors converge on four frames:
1. Cost against a ₹18k telecaller, made bigger by framing it as a 2–3-person coverage team.
2. Speed-to-lead, as a 30-second callback.
3. "AI employee" and 24/7 coverage.
4. Natural "Tenglish" with no language menu.

Adjacent SMB SaaS (Khatabook, Vyapar) grew on vernacular-first design, WhatsApp-native workflows, word-of-mouth between merchants, and freemium. Most of the adjacent evidence is secondary.

### Cited Findings
- VENDOR-CLAIM (Outpero, 14 Oct 2026). Its frames:
  - "AI employees";
  - the fair comparison is cost per covered hour, not one salary;
  - recruitment of ₹5k–20k per hire, 2–6 week ramp-up, attrition;
  - a 30-second callback, conceding it depends on how fast the lead source delivers;
  - everyday spoken Telugu with English switching, and callers never asked to choose a language. — [Outpero](https://www.outpero.com/blog/telugu-ai-voice-agent-vs-human-telecaller-cost); [Outpero Telugu page](https://outpero.com/telugu-ai-voice-agents)
- VENDOR-CLAIM. Low-price anchors:
  - NaCaller "from ₹3/min", no setup fee, 10 languages — [NaCaller](https://nacaller.com/)
  - Resonera ₹5/min flat plus 100 free minutes a month — [Resonera](https://resonera.ai/ai-calling-in/telugu)
- VENDOR-CLAIM (MyOperator). Frames are outcome case studies: an ashram's response time fell from 24–48 hours to 30 seconds, a hotel saw 80% less manual call handling and 50% more direct bookings, an agency doubled lead capture, and none of them added headcount. — [MyOperator](https://myoperator.com/blog/how-indian-smbs-use-ai-agents-2026-myoperator-deployments)
- NEWS (Inc42). Tata Communications pushes voice AI to SMBs with outcome-based, subscription pricing. — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/tata-communications-expands-voice-ai-push-eyes-smb-adoption/)
- SECONDARY (Khatabook, Inc42 profile about 7 years old). Word-of-mouth was the core acquisition channel, because shopkeepers believe other shopkeepers about savings. WhatsApp/SMS payment reminders were built into the product. — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/startups/sequoia-surge-backed-khatabook-is-helping-indian-shopkeepers-get-cashback/)
- SECONDARY BLOGS:
  - Khatabook is in 13+ vernacular languages and targets tier-2/3. — [businessmodelcanvastemplate](https://businessmodelcanvastemplate.com/blogs/marketing-strategy/khatabook-marketing-strategy)
  - Vyapar uses freemium-to-paid on GST billing, bills shared on WhatsApp, and YouTube/Facebook how-to content. — [Vyapar target market](https://businessmodelcanvastemplate.com/blogs/target-market/vyapar-app-target-market)
  - A GitHub research note says Khatabook/Vyapar field-sales playbooks are poorly documented and flags door-to-door demos as unverified. — [GitHub issue](https://github.com/vanshulgoyal101/adbrain/issues/1941)

### Inferences
- "Replace a telecaller at ₹18k" and "30-second callback" are now table stakes and no longer differentiate. Calevate's distinct material is compliance, follow-up journeys, the CRM and WhatsApp follow-up, and the dashboard assistant. None of the competitor pages read here leads with these.
- Khatabook's word-of-mouth pattern suggests that proof from a peer in the same trade and town beats vendor claims. That is an inference from old secondary evidence.

### Gaps
- UNKNOWN: Zoho, Razorpay, Interakt and Exotel SMB messaging. Not reached within the call budget.
- UNKNOWN: which frame actually converts better. No A/B data or conversion-by-message study found.

## 4. Proof formats that move SMBs

### Takeaway
Vendors offer sample calls, free credits or minutes, and live demos. Buyer guides tell owners to test with their own call scenarios and to interrupt the agent. There is no evidence ranking the formats against each other.

### Cited Findings
- VENDOR-CLAIM. Formats on offer:
  - Outpero: "Hear sample calls", 50 free credits with no card, "Talk to us"; no public demo number on the cost page. — [Outpero](https://www.outpero.com/blog/telugu-ai-voice-agent-vs-human-telecaller-cost)
  - Resonera: 100 free minutes a month. — [Resonera](https://resonera.ai/ai-calling-in/telugu)
- OPINION (JoyzAI buyer's guide). Test with real customers or representative sample calls, interrupt mid-sentence, and measure qualification rate, escalation rate and cost per qualified lead. — [JoyzAI](https://joyz.ai/blog/best-ai-voice-calling-agent-in-telugu/)
- OPINION (caller.digital). Buyers should insist on a 30-day pilot and a walk-away clause within 60 days. — [caller.digital](https://caller.digital/voice-ai-pricing-india)
- ANECDOTE. The founder's first vendor set-up was "a disaster" on batch one, and the system improved through about 50 iterations in 30 days. A first impression from a raw demo may understate the product. — [X post](https://x.com/Yash912/status/2041917124046991780?lang=en)

### Inferences
- A live Telugu demo number that the owner can call, playing the role of their own customer, directly answers the "does it understand our Telugu, and can I interrupt it?" test that buyer guides recommend. This is inferred, not measured.

### Gaps
- UNKNOWN: evidence on money-back guarantees, CA or trade-association endorsements, or local references for AI calling in AP/Telangana.

## 5. Pricing perception: per-minute vs flat vs outcome, telecaller comparison, GST

### Takeaway
Telugu-capable AI calling is listed at about ₹3–6/min (vendor price pages). Hyderabad telecallers earn about ₹12k–20k/month fixed pay (job boards). Competitors therefore anchor on "₹10–13k of AI vs ₹18k+ per human". Vendors add the hidden-fee fear to that anchor. Outpero shows 18% GST explicitly. There is no survey of owner preference between per-minute, flat and outcome pricing.

### Cited Findings
- JOB-BOARD DATA (WorkIndia, 2026 Hyderabad listings). Telecaller fixed pay is about ₹12k–15k for freshers and ₹15k–20k with 1+ years. Collection roles pay more. — [WorkIndia](https://www.workindia.in/telecalling-jobs-in-s-r-nagar-hyderabad/)
- VENDOR-CLAIM (Outpero). Its worked example: 2,000 min × ₹5 + ₹649 number = ₹10,649 before GST, about ₹12,566 with 18% GST. It compares this with ₹18k per telecaller, or ₹36k–54k for 2–3 hires. Premium voices are ₹6.8/min. — [Outpero](https://www.outpero.com/blog/telugu-ai-voice-agent-vs-human-telecaller-cost)
- VENDOR-CLAIM. Other price points:
  - JoyzAI ₹4/min + ₹1,100/month number + ₹10,000 setup;
  - Sarvam ₹3.50/min;
  - Bolna from ₹5.52/min;
  - Ringg ₹6/connected min + ₹499/month number. — [JoyzAI](https://joyz.ai/blog/best-ai-voice-calling-agent-in-telugu/)
- VENDOR-CLAIM (caller.digital). Market rates:
  - ₹3–6/min mid-market;
  - outcome-based ₹8–25 per outcome, but usually only at 50k+ calls/month;
  - a tier-2 BPO agent at ₹18k–25k/month works out to about ₹7–13 per connected call, "comparable" to AI per call. — [caller.digital](https://caller.digital/voice-ai-pricing-india)
- ANECDOTE: "Rs 1 to Rs 5 per minute. A 2-minute call costs less than Rs 10", compared with a ₹20k/month human making 80 calls a day. — [X post](https://x.com/Yash912/status/2041917124046991780?lang=en)
- VENDOR-CLAIM (PayU). UPI is about 85% of digital payment volume, and UPI AutoPay mandates passed 1.27 billion in one month in late 2025. — [PayU](https://payu.in/blog/?p=15779)
- OPINION (Mewayz, unsourced). A ₹3,999/month subscription is 2.5–8% of a tier-2/3 owner's personal draw. — [Mewayz](https://mewayz.com/blog/how-indian-smbs-buy-software-2026)

### Inferences
- Calevate's ₹4.50–6.00/min sits at the upper middle of listed Telugu rates, so pure price is not a winning frame. Two things are defensible: "no monthly fee, no number fee, no setup fee" as a fact-check against fees vendors list (JoyzAI's ₹10k setup, Outpero's ₹649/month number), and the all-in, GST-shown total. Whether Calevate charges a number fee was not verified here, so check before claiming it.
- The telecaller comparison only holds at the right volume. At ₹5/min, ₹15k buys 3,000 minutes. An owner whose volume is 500 minutes a month is comparing ₹2,500 with a salary. That frame helps for low-volume shops too, but as "cheaper than a part-timer", not "replaces an employee".

### Gaps
- UNKNOWN: an SMB survey on per-minute versus flat monthly preference, prepaid-pack acceptance, or GST invoice/ITC expectations specific to AI calling.

## 6. Sales process norms

### Takeaway
Evidence on in-person demos, WhatsApp follow-up selling and owner-only decisions for Indian SMB software is anecdotal or secondary. What exists points to word-of-mouth, WhatsApp-native support, UPI payment and freemium trials.

### Cited Findings
- SECONDARY: Khatabook used word-of-mouth between merchants. — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/startups/sequoia-surge-backed-khatabook-is-helping-indian-shopkeepers-get-cashback/)
- OPINION: the expected WhatsApp reply time is under 30 minutes in business hours. — [Mewayz](https://mewayz.com/blog/how-indian-smbs-buy-software-2026); WhatsApp named as a major SaaS support channel — [embedworkflow](https://embedworkflow.com/blog/selling-saas-in-india/)
- SURVEY, dated and not SMB-focused (G2 India 2022). Top buying factors are scalability, ease of implementation and ROI within 6 months; 89% have buying committees. — [G2](https://research.g2.com/india-buyer-behavior-study?hsLang=en)
- VENDOR-CLAIM (MyOperator): successful SMBs had no AI team and started with one narrow bottleneck. — [MyOperator](https://myoperator.com/blog/how-indian-smbs-use-ai-agents-2026-myoperator-deployments)
- VENDOR-CLAIM (caller.digital): Bolna is API-led and fits teams with developers rather than owner-operators. — [MyOperator top 10](https://myoperator.com/blog/top-10-voice-ai-agents-india-2026)

### Inferences
- Sell one bottleneck first, such as missed calls after hours or a callback to every new enquiry, rather than a platform. This is consistent with MyOperator's deployment pattern.

### Gaps
- UNKNOWN: evidence that AP/Telangana owners require in-person demos, or on typical decision time and payment terms for this category.

## 7. Evidence-backed positioning options and pitch elements

### Takeaway
Calevate's compliance and disclosure choices line up with the strongest consumer data available: 67% of Indians expect AI disclosure, and SMBs rank compliance and customer distrust as their #2 AI worries. Competitors lead with cost and speed instead. Positioning should lead with a business outcome on warm or inbound calls and use honest AI as the trust answer to "will my customers hate it?". Recommendations below are inferences; each lists its evidence.

### Cited Findings (evidence each option rests on)
- **Option A, "Answer every enquiry, honestly":** inbound and warm callbacks, with the agent saying it is the business's AI assistant.
  - 67% expect disclosure — [Ipsos](https://www.ipsos.com/en-in/trust-and-transparency-emerge-key-cornerstones-ai-usage-among-indians-ipsos-ai-monitor-2025)
  - customer distrust is a top-2 SMB AI concern — [CRN India](https://www.crn.in/?p=68989)
  - 6% hang-up on warm leads, with relevance mattering more than the voice — [X post](https://x.com/Yash912/status/2041917124046991780?lang=en)
- **Option B, "Not another spam caller":** no cold calls, consent, DNC respected.
  - 4,168 crore spam calls in 2025 — [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/india/story/indians-received-over-4000-crore-spam-calls-in-2025-1189-crore-blocked-report-515765-2026-02-11)
  - about 90% get pesky calls despite DND — [BOOM](https://www.boomlive.in/web-stories/90-of-indians-receive-spam-calls-despite-being-in-the-dnd-list-study)
  - compliance is a top SMB AI concern — [CRN India](https://www.crn.in/?p=68989)
- **Option C, "Pay only for minutes used, the total shown with GST":** a transparent price as the answer to the hidden-fee fear vendors themselves name.
  - the 2–4× hidden-cost claim and the list of fees — [caller.digital](https://caller.digital/voice-ai-pricing-india)
  - setup and number fees at competitors — [JoyzAI](https://joyz.ai/blog/best-ai-voice-calling-agent-in-telugu/); [Outpero](https://www.outpero.com/blog/telugu-ai-voice-agent-vs-human-telecaller-cost)
- **Option D, "Your staff do the closing; the agent does the first call and the follow-ups":** a hybrid model, not replacement.
  - zero added headcount in the case studies — [MyOperator](https://myoperator.com/blog/how-indian-smbs-use-ai-agents-2026-myoperator-deployments)
  - hybrid framing — [Outpero](https://www.outpero.com/blog/telugu-ai-voice-agent-vs-human-telecaller-cost)
  - 55% of Indians expect AI to help jobs — [Ipsos](https://www.ipsos.com/en-in/trust-and-transparency-emerge-key-cornerstones-ai-usage-among-indians-ipsos-ai-monitor-2025)
- **Proof elements:**
  - a Telugu demo the owner calls and can interrupt — [JoyzAI](https://joyz.ai/blog/best-ai-voice-calling-agent-in-telugu/)
  - free trial minutes, as competitors offer — [Resonera](https://resonera.ai/ai-calling-in/telugu); [Outpero](https://www.outpero.com/blog/telugu-ai-voice-agent-vs-human-telecaller-cost)
  - a same-trade peer case study, after Khatabook's word-of-mouth — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/startups/sequoia-surge-backed-khatabook-is-helping-indian-shopkeepers-get-cashback/)
  - a per-business rupee calculator against a ₹12–20k telecaller — [WorkIndia](https://www.workindia.in/telecalling-jobs-in-s-r-nagar-hyderabad/)

### Inferences
- Avoid leading with "AI employee", "30-second callback" or "native Telugu voices". They are competitors' frames and give no differentiation. Telugu quality should be shown through the demo, not claimed.
- Do not copy the "make it sound more human so people don't notice" tactic from the founder anecdote. It conflicts with Calevate's disclosure invariant and with the 67% disclosure expectation.
- Honest AI disclosure is supported by consumer expectation (Ipsos). Whether disclosure lowers or raises completion rates in Telugu is not measured, so do not claim "customers prefer an honest AI" as a statistic.

### Gaps
- UNKNOWN: any conversion data on these messages for AP/Telangana SMBs. These options are hypotheses to test with trial users, not proven pitches.
- UNKNOWN: first-hand Telugu owner quotes. They need direct Reddit/YouTube/Google-review collection or founder-run interviews.
