# Indian AI voice-agent competitor landscape for SMBs (as of 30 Oct 2026)

All pages were read on 30 Oct 2026 unless a different date is given. "CLAIM" marks a vendor's marketing statement that nobody has measured. "SNIPPET" means the figure came only from a search-engine summary and the full page was not read, usually because it returned 403. Prices exclude GST unless stated. UNKNOWN means the vendor does not publish the figure.

Coverage note: I read the primary pricing pages of Bolna, Ringg, Sarvam, Smallest.ai, Outpero and OmniDimension (the last as a snippet), plus Gnani's homepage. I did not read primary pages for Exotel, Ozonetel, Knowlarity, Haptik, Yellow.ai, SquadStack, Rezo, Subverse, CallHippo, Convin, Unpod, Vocallabs, VoiceGenie or Superbot. Their figures below come from third-party or competitor-authored sources and are labelled that way.

## 1. Who the players are, and SMB vs enterprise

### Takeaway
The products an Indian SMB can actually sign up for themselves are Bolna, Ringg, Sarvam's voice agents, Smallest.ai Atoms, OmniDimension, Outpero, Vyora and Bolti. Gnani, Haptik, Yellow.ai, SquadStack, Exotel, Ozonetel and MyOperator are sold through sales teams and pitched at enterprise or mid-market buyers. Most of the self-serve tools are developer-flavoured builders. Only Outpero, Vyora and Calevate package the product as a business tool for a non-technical SMB owner.

  - Raised a $6.3M seed in Jan 2026, led by General Catalyst, with YC, Blume, Orange Collective, Pioneer Fund, Transpose and Eight Capital.
  - CLAIMS more than 1,050 paying customers, over 200,000 calls a day (up from about 1,500 a day in May 2025) and a presence in more than 10 countries.

- **Funding wave, Jan 2026**: Arrowhead raised a $3M seed led by Stellaris (Entrackr, via [Ravan/Tracxn snippet](https://tracxn.com/d/explore/voice-ai-startups-in-india/__s7thq7EI12tPI5Mmcnok_iuzpK5VWI3-4ZhUxzXfMmA)).
- **Aggregate funding figures conflict**: Ravan reports about $329M across 5 rounds to Aug 2026, Tracxn about $33.9M to Sep 2026. Treat both as unreliable.

### Inferences
- The enterprise players (Gnani, Haptik, Yellow, SquadStack, Ozonetel) are not real alternatives for a 1–20 seat Telugu SMB. Their sales cycles, quotes and FDE-led deployments price SMBs out.
- The real SMB fight is with the self-serve builders (Bolna, Ringg, Sarvam, Smallest, OmniDimension, Bolti) and the SMB-packaged tools (Outpero, Vyora, MyOperator).
- The builders need someone to write prompts and wire up telephony. That is the gap Calevate's "business tool" packaging addresses.
- Sarvam is the most dangerous on price and Telugu. It is an Indian-language specialist with self-serve rates of ₹3.00–3.50 a minute.

### Gaps
- Not researched with primary sources: Rezo.ai, Subverse, CallHippo AI, Convin, Unpod, Vocallabs, VoiceGenie, Superbot, Tata Tele Smartflo AI, Plivo/Twilio India AI, and Knowlarity SuperReceptionist. Searches for VoiceGenie and Superbot returned nothing.
- Ringg's funding article returned 403, so the date and amount are snippet-only.

## 2. Published pricing (INR), trials, numbers and concurrency

Self-serve headline rates now cluster between ₹3 and ₹6 a minute. Sarvam is the cheapest with Telugu included, at ₹3.50 pay-as-you-go plus ₹0.40 a minute of telephony and ₹59 per number. Calevate's Clear at ₹4.50 is mid-pack. Studio at ₹6.00 matches Ringg and Bolti, and the Pilot rates of Bolna and Sarvam undercut it. Outpero has added a Custom tier from ₹3.5 a minute and 50 free credits.

- **Sarvam billing terms**:
  - Agent minutes are billed in 60-second pulses, rounded up, so a 61-second call is 2 minutes.
  - Telephony is billed in 30-second pulses.
  - Unconnected calls are free, but connected voicemail is billed.
  - Each simulated test run costs ₹2.
  - Source: [docs.sarvam.ai](https://docs.sarvam.ai/voice-agents/pricing.md)

### Inferences
- **All-in comparison** (my arithmetic from the published pages, approximate):
  - Sarvam PAYG with its own telephony is about ₹3.50 + ₹0.40 = ₹3.90 a minute, before 60-second rounding and GST.
  - On a 1.5-minute call, Sarvam bills 2 agent minutes, so about ₹7.80 a call. Ringg (per-second billing) and Outpero (connected talk-time) come out lower per call.
  - Per-second versus 60-second pulse billing is a real differentiator for short SMB calls. Calevate should state its own billing pulse.
- **Number rental**: Sarvam ₹59, Ringg ₹499, Outpero ₹649, Smallest $10. Calevate's number price is a lever against Outpero and Ringg.
- **Where Calevate sits**:
  - Clear at ₹4.50 is cheaper than Bolna PAYG (₹5.52 plus telephony), Outpero Standard (₹5) and Ringg (₹6).
  - It is dearer than Sarvam (about ₹3.90 all-in) and than Bolna Pilots and Outpero Custom (about ₹3.5–4).
  - Studio at ₹6.00 is priced against Outpero Premium (₹6.8), so it undercuts the closest premium competitor by ₹0.80.

## 3. Features: inbound/outbound, campaigns, WhatsApp, CRM, workflows, Telugu, cloning, transfer, latency

### Takeaway
Telugu is now table stakes: Bolna, Ringg, Sarvam, Outpero, Bolti, MyOperator and Gnani all list it. WhatsApp is bundled by Outpero and Ringg (as a separate ₹2 product) and by MyOperator, and Sarvam offers it only on Enterprise. Few self-serve tools advertise a built-in CRM with lead extraction. Latency figures are all vendor CLAIMS.

### Cited Findings
- **Outpero**:
  - Every tier includes instant leads and bulk campaigns, inbound, caller memory, in-call actions, WhatsApp, calendar and custom API integrations, recordings, summaries and captured fields, and auto-retries and callbacks.
  - It supports 7 languages, named as Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Hindi and Marathi (the seventh is not named).
  - Its page does not mention a CRM.
  - Its worked example: 2,000 calls × 1.5 min costs about ₹15,649 plus GST, about ₹8 a call.
  - Source: [outpero.com/pricing](https://www.outpero.com/pricing)
- **Sarvam**:
  - Supports 11 Indian languages plus English, including Telugu, with code-mixed speech.
  - Workflows, WhatsApp campaigns, custom tool authoring and forward-deployed engineers are Enterprise only.
  - Custom SIP is available on Business and above.
  - Source: [docs.sarvam.ai](https://docs.sarvam.ai/voice-agents/pricing.md)
- **Ringg**:
  - Has its own Indian-tuned STT ("Parrot"), a fine-tuned "4.1-class" LLM and TTS.
  - Sells chat, WhatsApp and browser agents as separate products, and offers bring-your-own LLM keys.
  - Basic analytics are included; advanced analytics cost ₹2 a call.
  - The pricing page names no CRM integrations.
  - Source: [ringg.ai/pricing.md](https://www.ringg.ai/pricing.md)
- **Bolna**:
  - Offers 10+ vernacular languages, including Telugu, plus a no-code playground and API, bulk campaigns, human transfer and BYOK. [bolna.ai/pricing](https://www.bolna.ai/pricing); [MyOperator](https://myoperator.com/blog/top-10-voice-ai-agents-india-2026)
  - CLAIMS India-hosted servers for lower latency. [Bolna docs](https://www.bolna.ai/docs/indian-server-configuration)
  - It is a Cartesia customer. [Cartesia](https://cartesia.ai/customers/bolna)
  - Third-party directories claim both sub-500ms and sub-300ms latency, which conflict and are unmeasured.
- **Gnani**:
  - CLAIMS 40+ languages including Telugu, "<200ms P95" latency, voice cloning, passive voice biometrics, and on-prem or air-gapped deployment.
  - CLAIMS "<20 mins" to go-live.
  - Its homepage does not mention WhatsApp.
  - Source: [gnani.ai](https://gnani.ai/)
- **Smallest.ai**:
  - Its Electron V2 TTS CLAIMS 53 ms time-to-first-token.
  - English and Hindi are production-ready, with English-Hindi code-switching. It CLAIMS 30+ languages, but Telugu was not confirmed. [TechPillow](https://www.techpillow.co/blog/smallest-ai-series-a-13-million-voice-india-2026)
  - Atoms offers a web widget and voice cloning (under Models). Custom integration is Enterprise only. Enterprise CLAIMS a 99.99% SLA, SSO and SOC 2. [smallest.ai/pricing](https://smallest.ai/pricing)
- **Yellow.ai**: CLAIMS voice cloning and 150+ integrations. [MyOperator](https://myoperator.com/blog/top-10-voice-ai-agents-india-2026)
- **Vyora**: CLAIMS TRAI/UCC compliance and DND scrubbing, and agent setup in "under 5 minutes". Recordings, analytics and CRM are missing from the Builder plan. [MyOperator](https://myoperator.com/blog/top-10-voice-ai-agents-india-2026); [GetApp](https://www.getapp.com/all-software/a/vyora-ai/)
- **Caller Digital**: Shopify, Zoho and Salesforce integrations. [MyOperator](https://myoperator.com/blog/top-10-voice-ai-agents-india-2026)
- **Hindi latency anecdote**: one write-up measured about 800ms per turn in English versus 2.5–4s per turn in Hindi before switching to Sarvam streaming. This is a single anecdote. [growwstacks](https://growwstacks.com/blog/sarvam-ai-tts-stt-voice-agent-test)

### Inferences
- **Calevate's distinct bundle**: CRM with schema-driven lead extraction, plus WhatsApp follow-ups, plus instant new-lead calls, at no monthly fee, in Telugu. None of the self-serve builders publishes that combination. Outpero is closest, but its page names no CRM.
- **What competitors offer that Calevate may not**:
  - Gnani: voice biometrics, on-prem deployment.
  - Ringg: chat and browser agents, and an evals product.
  - Bolna and Ringg: BYOK.
  - SquadStack: managed human-telecaller handoff.
  - Vyora: DND scrubbing marketed as a feature.
  - Smallest: a web widget.
  - Caller Digital: outcome-based (per-result) pricing.

### Gaps
- Telugu quality has not been compared independently. No vendor publishes a Telugu WER or latency figure.
- Human-transfer support is confirmed only for Bolna, from a secondary source.

## 4. Reviews, complaints and gaps to exploit

### Takeaway
Independent user reviews are scarce. G2 returned 403, Capterra listings show 0 ratings, and Reddit threads were not reachable through search. Most "comparison" content is written by competitors (MyOperator, Caller Digital, Ringg, OmniDimension, Edesy). The exploitable themes are opaque all-in pricing, telephony excluded from headline rates, developer-oriented setup, and features locked behind Enterprise tiers.

### Cited Findings
- Caller Digital says Indian voice-AI pricing is "structurally opaque" and that effective cost runs at 2–4× the headline rate. [caller.digital](https://caller.digital/blog/voice-ai-vendor-pricing-teardown-india-2026)
- Bolna's rate card excludes telephony. [caller.digital](https://caller.digital/blog/voice-ai-vendor-pricing-teardown-india-2026); [bolna.ai/pricing](https://www.bolna.ai/pricing)
- Ringg's blog (a competitor source) says Gnani typically needs its internal engineering team to configure, train and deploy agents, with no clear rate framework. [Ringg blog](https://www.ringg.ai/blogs/gnani-ai-reviews) (SNIPPET)
- An OmniDimension post summarising Reddit says code-mixed speech and rural accents remain unsolved in production. It also describes a Vapi bot that wrongly told a caller a restaurant was "fully booked". [omnidim.io](https://omnidim.io/blogs/voice-ai-platforms-reddits-most-asked-questions) (competitor summary)
- Sarvam bills in 60-second pulses and charges ₹2 per test run. Its workflows and WhatsApp are Enterprise only, from 2 lakh minutes a month. [docs.sarvam.ai](https://docs.sarvam.ai/voice-agents/pricing.md)
- Ringg requires 30 days' notice to cancel and bills silence and hold time. [ringg.ai/pricing.md](https://www.ringg.ai/pricing.md)
- Vyora's Builder plan lacks recordings and analytics, and its CRM is "coming soon". [GetApp](https://www.getapp.com/all-software/a/vyora-ai/)

### Inferences
- **Messaging angles Calevate can defend** (each must be true of Calevate before use):
  - One all-in per-minute price with telephony included and no monthly fee.
  - WhatsApp follow-ups and CRM included at SMB scale, not locked behind an Enterprise tier.
  - Telugu-first setup with no developer needed.
  - A transparent billing pulse.
  - No cancellation notice period.
- **Sarvam's ₹3.50 headline is the main price threat**: build the answer around all-in cost per call, not per-minute rate.

### Gaps
- No primary G2, Capterra or Reddit reviews were read: G2 returned 403 and Capterra has 0 ratings.
- Complaints about Outpero, Sarvam agents, Bolna and Ringg could not be sourced independently.
- To close this, someone needs to read G2, Capterra, Reddit and LinkedIn directly in a browser, or run test calls on each product.
