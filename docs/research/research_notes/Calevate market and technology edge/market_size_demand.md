# Market size and demand for AI voice agents / AI telecalling among Indian SMBs (AP + Telangana focus), October 2026

All sources were read on 30 Oct 2026. Evidence classes:
- **OFFICIAL**: government or regulator data.
- **ANALYST**: a paid market-research firm's estimate. Methodologies are opaque, and these figures are not evidence of spend.
- **SECONDARY**: a blog or news report repeating something else.
- **VENDOR-BLOG**: a competitor's marketing content.

Nothing here counts as verified unless the class says so. Budget note: about 17 tool calls were made. Many primary portals (sansad.in, marketsandmarkets, workindia) returned 403 errors or refused connections from this environment, so several figures below come from search-engine summaries of primary pages and are labelled that way.

## 1. India conversational / voice / contact-centre AI market size and growth (2024–2030); SMB vs enterprise share

### Takeaway
Analyst estimates for India *conversational AI* (chat and voice together) put 2030 at roughly US$1.8B–2.3B, with a 2024 base of about US$0.45–0.52B and a CAGR of about 26%. The one figure for *voice AI* specifically (US$153M in 2024 to US$958M in 2030) has no named analyst behind it. No source gives an SMB vs enterprise split. Treat all of these figures as directional only.

### Cited Findings
- **ANALYST, MarketsandMarkets (India conversational AI):** US$2,329.2M by 2030, CAGR 26.6%. This comes from a search-result summary of the [MarketsandMarkets India page](https://www.marketsandmarkets.com/Market-Reports/geography/conversational-ai-market/India); the page itself returned 403 to a direct fetch, so the base year was not read.
- **ANALYST, ResearchAndMarkets (India conversational AI):** INR 152.31 Bn by 2030, CAGR about 26.22% for 2025–2030 — [GlobeNewswire, 29 Aug 2025](https://www.globenewswire.com/fr/news-release/2025/08/29/3141434/28124/en/India-Conversational-AI-Business-Analysis-Report-2025-2030-Enhancing-Customer-Service-in-Banking-Via-Chatbots-and-Regional-Language-Support-Positioning-India-As-a-Global-AI-Leader.html) (search summary).
- **ANALYST, IMARC (India conversational AI):** US$516.8M in 2024, rising to US$4,936.9M by 2033 — [IMARC](https://www.imarcgroup.com/India-Conversational-AI-Market) (search summary).
- **ANALYST via VENDOR-BLOG, Grand View Research (India):** US$455.4M in 2024 and US$1,846.0M by 2030, CAGR 26.3%. This is quoted by caller.digital and **was not confirmed on grandviewresearch.com** — [caller.digital](https://caller.digital/blog/india-voice-ai-conversational-ai-market-size-segments-2026).
- **ANALYST, Grand View (global, for scale):** US$41.39B by 2030, CAGR 23.7% (2025–2030) — [Grand View press release](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/press-release/global-conversational-ai-market).
- **VENDOR-BLOG, India voice AI:** US$153.01M in 2024 rising to US$957.61M by 2030, CAGR 35.7%.
  - The post calls this "the published industry figure" but names **no analyst**.
  - The same post estimates that voice is roughly 25–40% of India conversational AI spend, offers a "commoditisation" scenario of about US$700M in 2030, and puts the BPO voice-process slice at US$12–15B.
  - Its vertical split (BFSI about 40% in 2024, D2C about 18%, healthcare about 12%) and deployment split (self-serve SaaS about 30% in 2024 rising to about 50% in 2030) are labelled "illustrative practitioner estimate" by the author.
  - It gives **no SMB vs enterprise split**.
  - Source: [caller.digital, 4 Jun 2026](https://caller.digital/blog/indian-voice-ai-market-size-153m-957m-growth-analysis-2030).

### Inferences
- The ANALYST range is consistent at about 26% CAGR. That consistency reflects shared desk-research methods, not independent confirmation.
- Taking the vendor's own 25–40% voice share and applying it to the about US$1.8–2.3B conversational total gives roughly US$0.45–0.9B of voice spend in 2030. That is the same order as the unattributed US$958M. Both inputs are estimates.
- These totals are dominated by enterprise spend: BFSI, telecom and e-commerce contact centres. The SMB share is unmeasured. Calevate should not quote any of these numbers as "our market" without that caveat.

### Gaps
- No source found gives an SMB vs enterprise split for India conversational or voice AI. **UNKNOWN.**
- No India-specific "contact-centre AI" (CCaaS AI) market figure was retrieved.
- NASSCOM, Redseer, Bain and KPMG reports on India GenAI or voice were not reached within the call budget.

## 4. Telecaller/BPO employment and spend; salaries in Hyderabad/Vizag; outsourced SMB telecalling spend

### Takeaway
Job-board data puts Hyderabad telecaller fixed pay at about **₹12,000–20,000 a month** in 2026: freshers ₹12–15k, one year or more of experience ₹15–20k, collections roles higher. The size of India's telecaller headcount and of outsourced SMB telecalling spend was not found.

### Cited Findings
- **SECONDARY (job board):** WorkIndia's Hyderabad pages state a typical ₹12,000–20,000 a month fixed pay. Individual postings include ₹15–18k (Cherlapally), ₹17–20k (collections, Neredmet) and ₹15–25k (collections, Balkampet). Sources: [WorkIndia Kapra](https://www.workindia.in/telecalling-jobs-in-kapra-hyderabad/) and related WorkIndia Hyderabad pages, via search summary; the direct fetch returned 403.
- **VENDOR-BLOG:** puts India's BPO voice-process slice at about US$12–15B, unattributed — [caller.digital](https://caller.digital/blog/indian-voice-ai-market-size-153m-957m-growth-analysis-2030).

### Inferences
These are cost-per-minute comparisons. Every input marked ASSUMPTION is not data.
- **A fully used telecaller is cheaper per talk-minute than Calevate's price.**
  - Inputs: ₹15,000 a month salary (midpoint of the cited band), 26 working days, 3 h of talk a day (ASSUMPTION).
  - Minutes: 3 × 60 × 26 = 4,680 talk-min a month.
  - Cost: ₹15,000 / 4,680 = **₹3.21 per talk-minute** on salary alone.
- **At lower use, the gap closes.** With 2 h of talk a day (ASSUMPTION): 3,120 min, so **₹4.81 per minute**.
- **At low SMB call volume, Calevate is cheaper.** At 1 h a day (ASSUMPTION): 1,560 min, so **₹9.62 per minute**.
- What the pitch can honestly say:
  - Calevate does not beat a busy, fully used telecaller on raw per-minute cost at ₹4.50–6.00 per minute.
  - It does win on idle time, after-hours and instant new-lead calls, attrition, and the salary-plus-overhead that these figures exclude (PF/ESI, seat, phone, supervision).

### Gaps
- Number of telecallers employed in India: **UNKNOWN**. NASSCOM and Naukri/Apna data were not reached.
- Vizag and Vijayawada salaries: **UNKNOWN**.
- Outsourced telecalling spend by SMBs: **UNKNOWN**. No source found.

## 6. Bottom-up serviceable-market estimate for Calevate in AP/TS

### Takeaway
This is an AP-only, assumption-driven range: about **₹42–210 crore a year** of addressable per-minute spend if 1–5% of AP's Udyam-registered MSMEs are qualified voice-lead businesses spending about ₹2,500 a month. The Telangana half cannot be computed until its Udyam total is read. The penetration share is the dominant uncertainty and is an ASSUMPTION.

### Cited Findings
- AP Udyam total 13,97,841 — the RS PQ above (OFFICIAL, via search summary).
- Price band ₹4.50–6.00 per minute — Calevate's own pricing, as given in the brief.
- TS RERA: 5,294 agents and 11,284 projects (SECONDARY, above).

### Inferences
Every percentage and volume below is an ASSUMPTION, labelled.

**A. AP, top-down from Udyam**

Inputs:
- N = 13,97,841 Udyam MSMEs in AP.
- q = share that are services or trade businesses that win customers by phone or WhatsApp and have enough call volume. ASSUMPTION, three scenarios: 1%, 3%, 5%.
- Usage = 500 min a month (ASSUMPTION) × ₹5.00 per minute (midpoint of ₹4.50–6.00) = ₹2,500 a month = ₹30,000 a year per customer.

Results:

| Scenario | Customers | Annual spend |
|---|---|---|
| q = 1% | 13,978 | 13,978 × ₹30,000 = ₹41.9 crore |
| q = 3% | 41,935 | ₹125.8 crore |
| q = 5% | 69,892 | ₹209.7 crore |

**B. Telangana real estate, a vertical slice that is countable today**

Inputs:
- Agents: 5,294. ASSUMPTION: 20% are active lead-buyers who call by phone, giving 1,059 customers × ₹30,000 = ₹3.2 crore a year.
- Developers: ASSUMPTION of 1 developer per 4 projects, giving about 2,821 developers from 11,284 projects. ASSUMPTION: 20% adopt at 2,000 min a month × ₹5 = ₹1.2 lakh a year, giving 564 × ₹1.2 lakh = ₹6.8 crore a year.
- Total for TS real estate: about ₹10 crore a year. This is assumption-heavy; the counts are secondary.

**C. Reading the numbers**
- Even the pessimistic AP case (about ₹42 crore a year) is large relative to an early-stage company. The binding constraint is sales reach and conversion, not market size.
- Shrink the market before pitching: replace q with a verified count per vertical (RERA, clinics, coaching, dealers) rather than a percentage of all MSMEs.

### Gaps
- Telangana Udyam total, needed to complete the AP+TS figure: **UNKNOWN**.
- Real minutes per SMB customer: no external benchmark was found. Calevate's own usage data is the right source once there are clients.
- Every penetration and adoption rate above is an assumption with no external source.
