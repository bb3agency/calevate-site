summaries (search engine reads the page and returns a summary); only the echai.ventures Zoho page was fetched in

reaches money, a pitch deck or a client-facing claim (CLAUDE.md hard rule 11). Agency/vendor blogs are commercially
interested and labelled as such. "ESTIMATE" = my inference; "UNKNOWN" = not found.

## 2. Partner / reseller channels

### Takeaway
Zoho's India model — zero entry fee, local IT-services firms in tier-2/3 cities adding a product line to existing
MSME relationships, plus a distributor (VAD) tier that recruits sub-resellers — is the documented template. Exact
Indian SaaS reseller margins are not published; the only figures are secondhand.

### Cited Findings
- Zoho partner tracks: consulting, GSI, value-added distributor (VAD), value-added reseller (VAR), affiliate,
  marketplace; VAD recruits and supports its own regional reseller network, called "unusual for SaaS" — [echai.ventures (fetched)](https://echai.ventures/gtm/gtm-channel-ecosystem/how-does-zoho-actually-reach-tier-2-and-tier-3-india-through-partners)
- Zoho tier-2/3 growth through distribution partners in Indore, Coimbatore, Jaipur, Guwahati; typical partner is "a
  local IT services firm already serving MSMEs in its city" — same source, citing DQ Channels
- Zero entry fee; tiers Authorised/Advanced/Premium; "margins reaching 40 percent in some categories" is secondhand,
  and Zoho's own partner page publishes no margins — same source
- Zoho partner counts conflict: "3,000-plus partners across 100 countries" (DQ Channels) vs "2,000-plus partners and
  9,000-plus affiliates" (Zoho page) — same source
- Zoho India commissions invoiced in INR via Zoho India; minimum payout INR 50,000 — [Zoho Store partner help](https://www.zoho.com/store/partner/help/receive-commission.html) (search-summary)
- Zoho affiliate commonly listed at 15% — [Post Affiliate Pro directory](https://www.postaffiliatepro.com/affiliate-program-directory/zoho-affiliate-program/) (third-party, search-summary)
- Zoho Mail reseller model: partner's commission comes off the full price, no separate commission — [Zoho Mail partner commission](https://zoho.com/mail/help/partnerportal/partner-commission.html) (search-summary)
- Zomentum founder (podcast, opinion): partner-led acquisition remains under-leveraged in India — via [echai.ventures](https://echai.ventures/gtm/gtm-partnerships/how-did-zoho-and-freshworks-build-partner-engines-that-carry-real-revenue-out-of-india)

### Inferences
- ESTIMATE: For Calevate, the analogous partner is a Hyderabad/Vijayawada/Vizag digital-marketing or real-estate
  marketing agency that already runs a developer's Meta lead campaigns — the AI agent calls those leads, so the agency
  sells it as a lead-conversion add-on. A 15-30% recurring revenue share sits between the Zoho affiliate and VAR
  figures above; this is a planning ESTIMATE, not a market rate.
- Per-minute pricing complicates reseller margin; a reseller card / wholesale-minute model (Zoho Mail style) is
  cleaner than commission payouts with high minimum thresholds.

### Gaps
- Telecom resellers and CA/consultant referral economics in AP/TS: UNKNOWN.
- Named Hyderabad Zoho partners or real-estate marketing agencies: not enumerated in this pass.
- Freshworks India partner margins: UNKNOWN (tiers only: Member/Silver/Gold/Platinum per echai).

## 3. Digital acquisition: ads, content, SEO, marketplaces

### Takeaway

Google CPCs for B2B SaaS in India are reported at ₹100-₹600+; Meta lead CPLs range ₹150-₹2,000 depending on vertical.
Competitors already run Hyderabad city pages naming Telugu and localities.

## 6. Benchmarks: sales cycles, trial-to-paid, churn

### Takeaway
No credible India-specific SMB SaaS benchmark was found. Global self-serve trial-to-paid is 15-25% (opt-in trials ~18%);
India figures (2-4% no card) come from one agency blog without methodology.

### Cited Findings
- India trial-to-paid 2-4% without credit card, 8-12% with card; monthly churn 5-8% — [upGrowth (agency, no methodology)](https://upgrowth.in/conversion-rate-benchmarks-for-indian-startups/)
- Opt-in free trial to paid 18.2%, opt-out 48.8% (First Page Sage data) — [Powered by Search](https://www.poweredbysearch.com/learn/b2b-saas-trial-conversion-rate-benchmarks/)
- Self-serve trial to paid 15-25% average, top quartile 30%+ (attributed to OpenView/ChartMogul) — [Sleek](https://www.getsleek.io/blog/saas-analytics-benchmarks-2026)
- SMB SaaS revenue churn often 3-9% of MRR per month (opinion post) — [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/i-built-a-free-churn-rate-calculator-for-saas-founders-heres-what-i-learned-about-churn-benchmarks-8143fa4bb3)

### Inferences
- ESTIMATE: plan with a 5-15% trial-to-paid range and 3-8% monthly logo churn until Calevate's own cohorts exist;
  usage-based (per-minute) revenue adds a usage-volatility churn that logo churn does not show.

### Gaps
- India SMB SaaS sales-cycle length: no source found. UNKNOWN.
- Primary ChartMogul/OpenView India cuts: not read.
