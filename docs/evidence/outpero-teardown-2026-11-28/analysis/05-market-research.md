## 3. Product patterns owners value in 2025–26

Most "owner love" evidence in this market is vendor-authored. G2 blocked automated reads in an earlier session (REPORTED, feature-audit 9 Nov). Reddit evidence reached me only second-hand, so the owner-sentiment lines below are labelled.

1. **Setup effort decides adoption.** Guides and vendor round-ups repeat that owners reject setup that takes over an hour or needs dialogue trees and API wiring. Outpero's describe-by-voice builder and Bolna's Agent Studio both answer this. (SECONDARY: [Brilo.ai roundup, which quotes Reddit second-hand: UNVERIFIED](https://www.brilo.ai/resources/best-ai-voice-agent-small-business); [OnceHub 2026 guide](https://www.oncehub.com/blog/best-ai-phone-agents-for-small-businesses-a-2026-buyers-guide))
2. **Caller trust and hang-up fear** are the most cited owner worry ("will they hang up when they hear it's AI"). It is answered by naturalness plus visible legitimacy. Bolna and Truecaller (brand, reason, AI watermark) is the first India product aimed at it. (SECONDARY / UNVERIFIED for the Reddit quote; READ-AT-SOURCE [CXOToday](https://cxotoday.com/media-coverage/bolna-and-truecaller-partner-to-bring-trust-and-transparency-to-ai-voice-calls/))
3. **Call simulation before go-live.** Retell has persona-based scenarios with criteria and mocks, runnable in CI. Vapi has testing on every plan. Sarvam prices it at ₹2 a run. No Indian SMB product makes it owner-facing. (READ-AT-SOURCE)
4. **QA scoring on every call.** SquadStack scores 23 parameters per call. Retell sells AI QA at $0.10/min. VoiceGenie is adding it in Jan 2027. (READ-AT-SOURCE)
5. **Human hand-off with context.** VoiceGenie shows the conversation on screen. SquadStack's model is a human pod. Exotel's Harmony has one supervisor watching many bots. G2 reviewers of Smith.ai praise smooth AI-to-human handoff. (READ-AT-SOURCE / SECONDARY [G2 Smith.ai](https://www.g2.com/products/smith-ai-ai-receptionists/reviews))
6. **Speed-to-lead.** Outpero calls a lead in about 30 s; SquadStack has its AI lead manager. The industry statistics on how fast a lead goes cold are widely quoted but **UNVERIFIED** here. I did not read a primary study, so do not use them in copy.
7. **WhatsApp as the second channel.** It is used for mid-call and post-call messages (Outpero, Vomyra) and for AI agents (Ringg at ₹2 per 5-min session, Haptik per conversation). The WhatsApp Business **Calling** API is in beta in India, with Exotel, Infobip and Engati as partners ([Exotel](https://exotel.com/blog/introducing-whatsapp-business-calling-api-beta/), [Meta docs](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/calling)). Whether it is generally available is UNVERIFIED.
8. **Missed-call call-back** is an Outpero inbound feature. It was not seen elsewhere in what I read.
9. **Voice cloning and voice choice:** Vomyra clones voices and lets you pick the model; Outpero merchandises named Telugu personas. Owners like to "hear the voice" before they buy.
10. **Fixed monthly cost.** Vomyra's "unlimited" plans and Haptik's conversation bundles show demand for a predictable bill, even with fair-use limits. Per-minute pricing stays the default ([Inc42](https://inc42.com/features/the-unit-economics-of-indias-voice-ai-boom/)).
11. **Outcomes over minutes.** Ringg's stated direction is agents that complete the task (bookings, cart recovery, KYC). Inc42 founders expect hybrid pricing: a base fee plus outcome incentives.

**What this means for the product:**
- **(a)** An outbound AI caller now has to treat number declaration, the series choice (140 or 1600) and verifiable consent or enquiry records as core features. Calevate's D-941 gate and the a2p filing work cover the first of these. Who counts as the "sender" on a ThinnestAI number is still open (OPERATIONS gate 59).
- **(b)** CNAP shows the KYC name of whoever owns the number. Clients' own-name numbers (D-693) become a pickup advantage.

## 6. Where Calevate can lead

These are opportunities that no vendor I read covers well for Indian SMBs:

1. **Owner-facing call rehearsal.** Simulation exists only as developer tooling (Retell, Vapi) or a priced API run (Sarvam ₹2). Nobody lets a shop owner hear five scripted callers (an angry one, a code-mixed Telugu one, a price haggler) against their agent before publishing, with plain-language pass or fail results.
2. **Published Telugu quality numbers.** No vendor publishes Telugu WER or latency (REPORTED, 30 Oct note; still true of every page I read). Measuring and publishing ours, with method, outranks "native dialect" adjectives.
3. **Trust on the handset as a bundle.** Combine:
   - CNAP showing the client's own KYC name (D-693 numbers);
   - a declared A2P number (D-941);
   - an AI and recording notice in the opening line (D-971).

   Bolna gets the AI label through Truecaller. Calevate can make "your business name shows on the caller's screen, and the call is declared and honest" a standard SMB promise. A Truecaller-for-Business integration is worth evaluating, but **UNVERIFIED** for SMB pricing.
4. **Compliance as a visible product, not a footnote.** It covers number declaration, 140 versus 1600 guidance, 7-day enquiry evidence kept per lead, verifiable consent records and a DND sync. Outpero and SquadStack only claim "DLT and consent checks" or "TRAI ready". Nobody shows the owner the evidence trail.
5. **QA scorecard on every call, included in the price.** SquadStack (enterprise) and Retell ($0.10/min) charge for or reserve QA scoring. An SMB-priced scorecard for each call, with "what the agent didn't know" fed back into the knowledge base, is open ground.
6. **Proof.** Outpero publishes no named customers. Real Hyderabad case studies with owner consent beat their "5,000+" figure.
7. **Honest bill preview.** Caller Digital shows headline and effective rates diverging two- to four-fold. A pre-campaign cost estimate counting pulses, retries and connect rate, plus a cost per completed conversation on the dashboard, would be unique among SMB vendors.
8. **WhatsApp as a real channel, not just a link.** Ringg and Haptik price WhatsApp agents. Outpero only sends messages. Use one knowledge base for calls and WhatsApp replies, and follow WhatsApp Calling (beta in India) as it matures.
9. **Partner programme with published terms.** Outpero's has no published terms. Vomyra's is a margin promise behind annual plans. Meta-lead agencies and BNI members in Hyderabad are reachable with a transparent revenue share and per-client workspaces (already in D-693).
10. **Programmatic SEO done honestly.** Answer the competitor estates with Telugu-first city × vertical pages and measured comparison pages, with sources cited, rather than vendor-claim tables.
