## Fixed

### E1-01 (P2): sending the address answer again added a second "Main branch"
- **Where:** `apps/api/first_run/service.py::save_answers` (the `answers.location` arm).
- **Scenario:**
  - `QuestionsStep.finish` posts the answers and then the build, as one press.
  - When the build is refused (a network drop, `first_run_builds_used`), pressing "Build my
    agent" again posts the answers again.
  - The same happens when the owner goes Back from the build and answers the questions again.
    An unchanged brief keeps the same questions.
  - Each send appended `Branch(label="Main branch", address=...)`. The business profile, which
    every agent reads, then held the same address twice.
- **Fix:** `_with_main_branch`.
  - An address already on file is left alone.
  - An existing main branch takes a corrected address.
  - Otherwise one main branch is appended.
- **Commit:** `e335c3c20`.
- **Test:** `tests/first_run_test.py::test_the_address_answer_sent_again_keeps_one_main_branch`.
  It FAILED on the old service (run with the old file in the lane container) and passes now.

### E1-02 (P2): used-up trial Studio minutes were a dead end at the test call
- **Where:** `apps/web/src/app/c/[slug]/agents/first/HearItStep.tsx` (`CallInProgress`).
- **Scenario:**
  - `refuse_trial_studio_over_cap` (`compliance/trial_access.py:303`) runs inside
    `dispatch_call`, under the trial-line lock. So `POST /v1/trial/calls` answers it with a
    422 `trial_studio_minutes_used`, not a `blocked` result. `trial_daily_cap` is raised the
    same way under that lock.
  - The trial panel (`GET /v1/trial`) carries no Studio figure, so nothing warns before the press.
  - The call stage drew every refusal as an error. It showed the detail alone ("This free
    trial includes 15 minutes of test calls on Studio voices.") and dropped the remediation.
  - Its "Try again" re-dialled into the same refusal.
  - So a trial owner on a Studio voice, which D-855 picks by default, had no way past it on screen.
  - Reachable when the trial's free minutes exceed `trial_studio_minutes`. Both default to 15
    (`calevate_shared/config.py:654,688`), but an operator's "Set new terms" can give more.
    It is also reachable when the Studio count, which includes unmetered call lengths, gets
    there first.
- **Fix:**
  - A refusal whose code is a trial limit is drawn in the limit frame. For the Studio rule it
    offers "Choose a Clear voice" (`?section=voice`) and "Add credit".
  - Every other refusal on the stage now shows its remediation.
- **Commit:** `e8c95dc8e`.
- **Test:** `tests/firstAgent.test.tsx` "shows used-up trial Studio minutes as a limit with its
  way forward ...". It FAILED on the old source and passes now.

### E1-03 (P2): the brief typed or spoken was lost on Back, on Pip, and on reload
- **Where:**
  - `apps/web/src/app/c/[slug]/agents/first/BriefStep.tsx` (box state);
  - `MeetYourAgent.tsx` (the step unmounts on every stage change).
- **Scenario:**
  - The owner types a description, or speaks pieces (at most three, `MAX_RECORDINGS`, with no
    slot given back; their words exist only in the box until Continue).
  - They then press Back to the job step, or "Talk it through with Pip". Coming back showed
    the old saved brief, or an empty box.
  - A reload lost the words with no warning.
- **Fix:**
  - The screen holds the unsaved draft across steps.
  - While the box is unsaved, the step registers `useUnsavedGuard`, which asks before a reload.
- **Commit:** `22d2ece8e`.
- **Test:** `tests/firstAgent.test.tsx` "keeps what was typed in the brief through Back and
  returning". It FAILED on the old source and passes now.

## Checked, no defect found

- **Signup:**
  - The vertical list matches `VERTICAL_TEMPLATES` plus `custom`.
  - The slug preview matches the server's 3-character floor, and the slug is asked for when
    the name cannot become one.
  - `email_not_verified` is a step with a button and a retry that keeps the form.
  - A closed or load-shed signup replaces the form.
  - The success panel's "Meet your agent" goes to `/c/<slug>/agents/first`.
- **Google sign-up:**
  - The callback goes to `/c`. A person with no workspace (403 `forbidden`) is sent to
    `/signup`. A due first run goes to `/agents/first`, and a due go-live guide to `/go-live`.
- **First-run server state:**
  - Every write is under the owner permission.
  - Questions and build carry stale guards (`QUESTIONS_STALE_AFTER`, `BUILD_STALE_AFTER`).
  - A repeated build while building is a no-op.
  - Job and language are locked after the build, with a remediation that names the page.
- **The ticks before the test call:** each unaccepted agreement, the pledge and "this number
  is mine" are separate ticks, accepted in sequence before `/prepare` and the dial.
- **Go-live guide:**
  - Each `NumberState` has a screen state.
  - The team step's card follows `GET /v1/go-live`.
  - A forwarded number needs a Calevate number (`forwarded_lines.calevate_number_id`), so
    the guide's count of `phone_numbers` is true for "buy or forward".
- **Verify your business on a trial:** the forms are shut (`TRIAL_SHUT`), not offered and then
  refused. The GSTIN and PAN patterns match the server, and the values are sent upper-cased.
- **First campaign:** the agent cards say "Answers calls only" for an inbound-only agent. Y4
  verified the launch checklist buttons, the upload counts and the duplicate-submit guards.

## NOT FIXED / UNVERIFIED

- **P3, reload mid-questions.** Answers are posted only at "Build my agent", and `FirstRunOut`
  carries no answers. A reload or Back during the questions shows them blank again. The server
  keeps answers already sent (`first_runs.answers`) but cannot pre-fill them. Fixing this
  needs a wire field, so it was left for a founder or UX call.
- **P3, stale "writing" questions.** `_questions_status` measures staleness from
  `first_runs.updated_at`. Any later write (saving the job again, adding a document) restarts
  the 10-minute window of a lost questions job. The owner can still "Skip the questions and
  build", so this is not a dead end.
- **P3, pledge read failure.** In `HearItStep`, a failed pledge read shows no pledge tick and
  no retry for it. The dial is then refused by the pledge rule, in the server's words.
- **P3, missing statement version.** In `HearItStep.ring`, `if (!statement) break;` skips the
  agreements when `acceptance_statement_version` is missing. `/prepare` then refuses in its
  own words, which the stage now shows with its remediation (E1-02).
- **UNVERIFIED, not walked.** The DigiLocker round trip, the number purchase and the campaign
  launch were not run end to end in a browser here. They were read only as far as above, and
  Y4, Y5 and Z2 cover them.
