## Method

1. Rememory first: memories for Z9, X4, D1, sweep-9 sw6, C-06 and the 10 Nov round, then every
   prior report on this path (Z9, X4, D1, D2, D2b, D5, Z3, Z4, Z7).
2. Ran `check_erasure_coverage` and `check_redaction_exposure` on the migrated lane DB (both OK
   at base: 69 subject-linked tables; subject arm reaches 25 + 12 registered, tenant arm 36 + 1).
3. The gate finds tables by COLUMN SHAPE (an `*e164*`/`phone`/`subject_ref`/`caller_ref`
   column, an FK to `calls`/`leads`, or a prose/jsonb column of a listed name). A table holding
   a person's data under another column name is invisible to it, so I listed every text,
   varchar, jsonb, text[] and bytea column of all 195 public tables from `pg_attribute` and
   read each candidate's producer and its erasure and retention path by hand.
4. Then I compared the three data-rights paths against each other: what §12 subject erasure
   empties, what the retention sweep ages out, and what the §11 subject export discloses.

## Fixed

### E7-01 (P2, compliance): the subject access export withheld what each call captured about the person

- **Where.** `apps/api/compliance/export.py::build_subject_export` (calls query and entries),
  `apps/api/compliance/export_routes.py::SubjectExportCallOut`.
- **Failure scenario.** §12 empties `call_extractions.data`, `call_extractions.unlisted`,
  `calls.goal_reason` and `calls.insights` for the person (`workers/retention.py`
  `execute_deletion_request`), and the client's own call screen shows all four (`CallDetailOut.
  extraction`, `insights`, `crm/captured.lead_unlisted`). The §11 export carried none of them:
  - the lead holds only the latest value of each detail, so an address captured on an earlier
    call and since replaced was held and shown to the business, but withheld from the person;
  - the caller's own words for a choice field nothing matched (`unlisted`) were withheld too;
  - so were the objections and problems they raised, in their own words (`insights`).

  An access answer that under-reports is the defect `export.py` already names in its own
  `_ERASURE_SQL` comment.
- **Fix.** Each call entry now has these fields:
  - `captured`: that call's extraction, verbatim like `lead.data`, with the same reasoning,
    acknowledged in `check_redaction_exposure`;
  - `said`: masked;
  - `goal_reason`: masked;
  - `insights`: read by the call screen's own reader. `crm.service._insights_out` moved to
    `crm.schemas.insights_out` so that both use one function. The quoted texts are masked with
    `mask_foreign_numbers`, like every summary.
- **Test.** `tests/journey_audit_2026_11_10_e7_test.py::test_a_subject_export_discloses_what_each_call_captured_about_the_person`.
  It failed before the fix (`assert None == {'address': ...}`).

### E7-02 (P2, compliance): the export never listed the automated follow-ups the number is in

- **Where.** Same function; new `SubjectExportFollowUpOut`, `counts.follow_ups`.
- **Failure scenario.** §12 stops and forgets a person's Follow-up runs
  (`retention._erase_follow_up_runs`). A run records that this number is being worked through
  scheduled calls and messages, and it can exist with no lead at all (a run started from a date
  or a delivery: `uq_follow_up_runs_trigger_no_lead`). The export never read
  `follow_up_runs`, so a person in a sequence was told nothing about it, and with no lead they
  were told "we hold nothing".
- **Fix.** `follow_ups`: the follow-up's name, status, start, end and end reason, by number.
- **Test.** `::test_a_subject_export_discloses_the_follow_ups_the_person_is_in`. It failed before
  the fix ("the enrolment was not disclosed").

Legal: `/legal/privacy` §12.3 now lists the new contents. The revision is 28, not material, in
both `apps/web/src/lib/legal/versions.ts` (hash printed by `pnpm -C apps/web legal:hashes`) and
`apps/api/legal/catalogue.py`. The DPA's export sentence is phrased as "containing" and was left
as it is.

## NOT FIXED

- **E7-N1 (P3): derived copies still missing from the export.** These are emptied by §12 but not
  exported:
  - the knowledge-gap question quotes per call (`knowledge_gap_occurrences.question_redacted`);
  - `team_alerts.reasons`, which are owner-configured choice values matched on the call, not the
    caller's words (`team_alerts/models.py:15`);
  - test cases the owner saved from a call (`agent_test_cases.caller_lines`, owner-edited);
  - `save_number_messages`, deleted by the forwarding worker once used.

  The first is the only one in the caller's own words. It is a small addition to the same
  function, but I stopped at the two that the client's own screens show about the person.
- **E7-N2 (P3, retention): caller lines saved into a test case never expire.**
  `agent_test_cases.caller_lines` (and runs that copied them) come from
  `transcript_turns.text_redacted` (`teach/test_cases.draft_from_call`). Subject and tenant
  erasure reach them. The transcript clock does not, so the lines outlive the transcript they
  were copied from. They are owner-edited test material, so whether they belong on the
  transcript clock is a product question (FOUNDER DECISION 2).
- **E7-N3 (P3): Redis.** `incall:once:<sha256>` keeps an in-call action's answer for 3 h
  (`reliability/in_call_once.py:41`). The answer can hold a caller lookup result. Erasure does
  not reach it; the TTL bounds it. arq job arguments live only for the queue's lifetime.
  Neither was changed.
- Earlier rounds' open items X4-N1, X4-N2, Z9-N2 and D2b's follow-up race were not re-examined.

## FOUNDER DECISIONS

1. **Onward copies are not named on the erasure certificate.** `deletion.ERASURE_LIMITATIONS`
   names every processor it cannot reach ("misleading by omission"), but says nothing about
   copies Calevate has already delivered to the business's own connected tools:
   - outbound webhooks to their CRM;
   - LeadSquared (`workers/leadsquared_sync.py`);
   - Google Sheets rows written by actions (`actions/sheets.py`);
   - team alert emails and WhatsApp messages to staff;
   - WhatsApp follow-ups in the business's own account.

   We erase our stored delivery body (`_erase_delivery_bodies`), not the receiver's copy. The DPA
   (`apps/web/src/lib/legal/dpa.ts:397-408`) says the erasure reaches "the bodies delivered to
   your own systems", which is our stored copy only, but a reader can take it otherwise. A
   proposed added limitation: "Copies already delivered to this business's own connected tools
   (its CRM, spreadsheets, staff alerts and WhatsApp account) are in the business's own systems
   and are not deleted by this request; removing them there is the business's step."

   This changes client-facing certificate wording and the index-aligned `ERASURE_EXCEPTIONS`,
   so it is yours to approve. The DPDP §8(7) and Rule 8 erasure text was not read in its gazette
   form here: UNVERIFIED (secondary summaries only, below).
2. Should caller lines saved into a test case expire on the transcript clock (E7-N2)?
