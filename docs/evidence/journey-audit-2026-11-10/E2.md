## Fixed

### E2-01 (P2): a "call me later" heard only by the re-read of a failed reading booked nothing

- **Where.** `apps/workers/extraction_reread.py::reread_extraction` ran the pipeline's reading
  stages (summary, outcome, business outcome, lead, hot alert) but not STEP 3d' (the after-call
  call back, `pipeline.py` around old line 1699).
- **Failure.** The extraction provider times out on a call where the caller said "call me
  tomorrow". `extract_call` stores `errors._model` (by design, so the call, lead and minutes
  survive), and STEP 3d' needs a model's reading, so nothing is booked. The D-841 sweep reads the
  call again 30+ minutes later and the model now says `callback_requested`. The re-read records
  "Needs you" and stops: no call back is ever booked, and the caller who asked is never rung.
- **Fix.** STEP 3d' is now `pipeline.book_asked_callback`, called by the pipeline and by the
  re-read before the outcome (so a booked one reads "Call back booked"). Booking stays once per
  call (`book_requested_callback._already_has_one`).
- **Test.** `test_a_reread_that_hears_call_me_later_books_the_call_back`. Before the fix:
  `assert 0 == 1` call backs.

### E2-02 (P2): the team alert showed a hidden caller as the business's own number

- **Where.** `apps/workers/team_alerts.py::deliver_team_alert`,
  `phone = to_e164 if outbound else from_e164`.
- **Failure.** A forwarded call that showed the client's published number (D-746). The agent
  uses `tell_the_team` mid-call, or the owner's rule matches after the call. The alert, which
  carries the full number on purpose so the team can ring back, reads "Phone: <the business's own
  number>". The team rings their own line (which forwards back to the agent) and never reaches
  the caller. Mid-call the pipeline has not yet marked `caller_id_hidden`, so even reading that
  column would not have been enough.
- **Fix.** `_caller_phone`: outbound is `to_e164`; inbound that is marked hidden, or whose shown
  number classifies as `business_number_shown` against `published_numbers` (the rule the in-call
  actions already use, `worker/engine_actions._tool_call`), shows the lead's number (the lead of
  a hidden caller is filed under the call-back number they gave, `_upsert_lead`) or
  `HIDDEN_CALLER_PHONE` ("not shown, the call came through your own number").
- **Tests.** `test_a_hidden_caller_is_not_shown_to_the_team_as_the_business_number` (in-call,
  not yet marked) and `test_a_hidden_caller_who_gave_a_number_is_shown_by_that_number`. Before:
  both bodies held `Phone: +919900011122`.

### E2-03 (P2): a hand-over nobody took booked a call back to the business itself

- **Where.** `apps/workers/handoff.py::_book_callback_for` dialled `snapshot.from_e164`, and
  `pipeline._run_post_call_stages` ran STEP 1d (hand-over settle) BEFORE STEP 1e (which marks
  `caller_id_hidden`).
- **Failure.** A forwarded call that hid the caller asks for a person; nobody picks up. STEP 1d
  books the "nobody was able to take the call, we will ring them back" call back to the shown
  number, the business's own. The dispatcher rings and bills the business, and the caller is
  never called. Same class as Z4-03, on the hand-over path Z4 did not reach.
- **Fix.** STEP 1e now runs before 1d. The hand-over books through
  `after_call_callbacks.number_to_ring` (made public; one helper for "whom does a call back
  ring"), which answers None for a hidden caller with no given number and for our own forwarding
  test. The call reads "Needs you" (`derive_outcome`: a hand-over that reached nobody), and STEP
  3d' still books on the given number when the caller asked to be called.
- **Tests.** `test_a_handover_nobody_took_never_books_a_call_back_to_the_business` (before:
  `['+919900011122']` booked) and
  `test_the_pipeline_knows_the_caller_is_hidden_before_the_handover_settles` (before: the call
  read `caller_id_hidden = False` when the hand-over settled).

## Checked, no defect found (read at source)

- **Extraction refused / provider down.** `extract_call` returns `valid=False` with `_model`;
  the call, lead, meter and alerts still land; D-841 re-reads; the re-read never meters the call
  again or re-sends `call.completed` (sends `call.updated` only on a changed outcome).
- **Duplicate or re-run pipeline.** `_meter` returns early under `lock_call_writes`;
  `charge_for_call` dedupes on `ref = call_id`; the lead counts a call once (`call` timeline
  event); owner alert dedupes on `hot-lead:<lead>:<call>`; team alerts on
  `UNIQUE (call_id, contact_id)`; after-call actions and Follow-ups via `enqueue_outbox_once`;
  CRM fan-out via `crm_notified_at` under the lock.
- **Answered call with no transcript yet.** Waited for on the retry ladder (D-848); the last try
  settles and alarms `engine_call_answered_without_transcript`.
- **Owner alert channel down.** Email retried (15 s, 45 s), recorded once per lead+call on the
  timeline with `delivered: false`, alarmed when exhausted; no billing email alarms at once;
  WhatsApp has its own job and ladder. Team alert retried, `team_alerts.status = 'failed'`,
  audited and alarmed; Pip's diagnostics read failures.
- **Paused line / credit stop.** `override_call_script` sets `answersCalls: false` with
  `PAUSED_LINE_MESSAGE` (no AI speaks, nothing recorded, no recording notice claimed); the
  attention feed's `inbound_stopped` reads what the engine holds, not the balance.
- **In-call actions on a hidden caller.** `client_action` passes `caller_e164=None`;
  `in_call_tools._subject` returns `caller_number_hidden`, so no opt-out against the business and
  no call back to it.

## NOT FIXED / UNVERIFIED

- **E2-N1 (P3, design question).** A hidden caller who gave no call-back number has no lead, so
  `tell_everyone` tells the owner nothing (`lead_id is None`) even when the owner's rule matched;
  team addresses are told. If the account has no team address, the only trace is the call in the
  log (and "Needs you" when the outcome says so). Changing who is told needs the founder (D-967).
- **E2-N2 (P3).** On the first pass of a call whose agent used `tell_the_team` but whose reading
  failed, the owner is told with trigger "Needs a person" under the dedupe key
  `hot-lead:<lead>:<call>`. If the D-841 re-read later reads the call as hot, the owner's alert
  for that call has already gone, so the owner is not told it was hot (the team, already told
  in-call, is not told again by design). Not changed: it needs a decision on whether one call
  may alert the owner twice.
- **E2-N3 (P3, UNVERIFIED).** `_upsert_lead`'s `is_repeat_caller` excludes `trial_call` but not
  `paid_test_call` (founder decision 7: a test call never makes a repeat caller). Paid test calls
  are outbound to the owner's own phone (D-859), so this is outside the inbound journey and I did
  not build a failing case.
