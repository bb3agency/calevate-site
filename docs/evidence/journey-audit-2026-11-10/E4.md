## What was built

`tests/journey_audit_2026_11_10_e4_test.py::test_one_clients_money_walks_a_month_and_its_boundary_to_the_paisa`
walks one prepaid tenant in time order through the real writers. After each step it checks
that every screen figure agrees to the paisa:

- wallet balance;
- newest `balance_after`;
- sum of the ledger;
- open lots' `credits_remaining`;
- `spend_state` (minutes and billed rupees);
- `usage_summary` (spend, minutes, calls, Clear plus Studio charges);
- the spending-limit counters;
- the wallet drawdown (calls, AI help, rental, lapsed, refunded);
- the statement-list row (total, credit added, wallet spent, credit expired, calls);
- the statement document's lines.

The steps:

1. A free trial (`start_trial`). A call inside it is metered for minutes, charges ₹0, and
   counts ₹0 against the limit.
2. The first top-up: a signed `payment.captured` goes through the real webhook receiver. It
   opens a lot, the trial converts, and the counters restart.
3. Calls on both voices: one Clear, one Studio (a `sonic-3.5:` voice id), each through the
   real `pipeline._meter`.
4. AI help: the allowance is used up, then `purchase_ai_overage` buys the extra block.
5. A spending limit set below what is already spent. `check_dispatch` refuses with
   `spend_cap`.
6. A number's rental (`charge_number_rental`) for this IST month.
7. A second top-up whose lot lapses (`expire_lot` at its own Terms-19 `expires_at`).
   Lapsed credit leaves the balance and the lots and is not counted as spend.
8. The roll. The process clock of every billing reader is frozen at 00:00:01 IST on the 1st
   of the next month. Then:
   - A call that ended at 23:59:20 IST settles after the roll and lands in the closed month
     on every screen.
   - The new month's counters read zero, and last month's limit no longer stops a dial.
   - The AI allowance resets and the extra block can be bought again.
   - The closed month's statement keeps its number and its total, plus the late call.
   - The new month's statement is ₹0.
9. An admin refund of ₹100, confirmed by a signed `refund.processed` webhook. The money
   leaves as a refund, off the lot. It counts as neither spend nor a change to the closed
   month's statement.

The ledger is stamped by the database clock (`clock_timestamp()`), so money moves in the
real current IST month. The roll is taken on the process clock. That is the clock every
"which month is it now" reader uses (`current_billing_month`, `statement_page`,
`read_ai_quota`, `spend_capped`).

## Finding

### E4-01 (P2, money/statement): the extra AI help the wallet paid for was on no statement

- **Where:** `apps/api/billing/invoice.py::build_invoice`. Before the fix it listed the plan
  fee, one-time charges, rental, prepaid calls, overage and model upgrade. It had no line for
  `ai_quota.purchase_ai_overage`.
- **Failure:** an owner buys the extra block of AI help (₹500 by default,
  `Settings.ai_overage_block_inr`). That month's statement document and the "Total" column
  of the statement list both left it out. The "Wallet spent" column beside them included it
  (`wallet.DRAWDOWN_BUCKETS_SQL` counts the `ai_assist` debit). The wallet screen's "Where
  it went" also lists "Extra AI help". The measured result on the walk:
  - statement `total_inr` was ₹20.25;
  - `wallet_spent_inr` was ₹520.25.

  So a supply debited from the wallet appeared on no monthly bill of supply.
- **Fix:** new `ai_quota.ai_help_statement_lines(session, tenant_id, month)`. It reads the
  one block row a tenant-month can hold, by `overage_ref(month)` (the month the block bought
  allowance for), through the existing unlocked `read_entry_by_ref`. It returns one line,
  "Extra AI help", with qty 1 and the amount debited. `build_invoice` appends it after the
  calling lines. The label is the one the wallet screen already uses
  (`WhereItWent.tsx: "Extra AI help"`). The PDF/print view renders lines generically
  (`components/invoiceDocument.tsx`).
- **Test:** step 4 of the walk. Before the fix it failed with "the extra AI help the wallet
  paid for is missing from the month's statement" and `Decimal('20.25') == Decimal('520.25')`.
  It passes after.

## Checked along the path, no defect found

- **The trial boundary:**
  - `end_trial` zeroes `spend_state`;
  - `counter_epoch` floors the open and closed month's panel;
  - after conversion, the counter's minutes (4.00), the panel's minutes and the statement's
    call count agree;
  - the trial call is metered but not debited.
- **The webhook credit:**
  - the lot opens with the Terms-19 `expires_at`;
  - balance = lots = ledger after every step;
  - a refund takes the credit off the lot (`remove_credit_from_lots`).
- **Metering at the boundary:**
  - `ist_billing_month(ended_at)` places the late call;
  - `_CALL_IN_MONTH` puts its debit in the closed month's calling lines and `usage_summary`;
  - the `_SPEND_STATE_UPSERT` leaves the open month alone.
- **Rollover:**
  - `read_spend_counters` reads a closed-month row as zeros;
  - `spend_capped` compares the month;
  - `read_ai_quota` resets the allowance and the block;
  - `statement_page` lists the new month at ₹0 and marks the old one closed;
  - the invoice number is stable.
- **Auto-recharge monthly cap** (`_month_spent`): it counts by `created_at` in the IST
  month, and one charge stays pending (partial unique index). A charge created at 23:50 IST
  and captured after the 25-hour pre-debit lag counts against the month it was started in.
  That is a stated rule, not a defect. X1-03 and Z1-01 were not redone.

  Each has its own suite. Adding them to this walk is the next extension.
- **P3, observation only:** for a lapsed lot, credit stays spendable between `expires_at` and
  the daily 08:11 IST `expire_credit_lots` run, because `lots._SELECT_OPEN_LOTS` does not
  filter on `expires_at`. That is in the client's favour by up to a day, and a lapse
  recorded on the 1st belongs to the previous month's lot. Changing it touches the lapse
  rule, which another agent owns (rememory a959b34e), so it was left alone.
- **Lapsed-credit refund:** not touched, as the brief requires. Step 9 refunds from the
  unlapsed first payment for that reason.
