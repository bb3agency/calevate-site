# Make a call: one call to one person (5 Nov 2026)

The founder asked for this on 5 Nov 2026: "Currently the clients are only able to make calls through campaigns only. But we would need some backend and UI to make calls for single users at once at clients choice."

He then decided on Option 1. One sidebar section holds every one-off call, with one form and a list of recent one-off calls. Every other Call button opens that same form with the person already filled in. There is still only one way the backend places a call, `crm/lead_dial`, and Pip's `call_place` goes through the same function.

## 1. What already existed (read from the code on this branch)

**Dialling one lead now: `apps/api/crm/lead_dial.py`**
- `place_lead_call` handles `POST /v1/leads/{lead_id}/call`.
  - It requires an `Idempotency-Key` header.
  - It works in three transactions: the claim, then the gate and the dial, then the audit record and completion.
  - It writes a `lead.call_dispatched` audit row.
  - When the gate refuses, it answers 200 with `status: "blocked"`, a `blocked_reason` and a `blocked_rule`.
- `place_call_back` handles `POST /v1/calls/{call_id}/callback`.
  - Its key is the earlier call, scoped to the account.
  - It links the new call to the earlier one through `crm.service.link_callback`.
  - It carries the agent's own redacted summary as the reason for the call (`plan_callback`).
- `call_back_check` answers whether a call back is allowed. It serves `GET /v1/calls/{call_id}/callback`.

**The compliance gate: `apps/api/compliance/service.check_dispatch`**

It checks these, in order:
1. The big red switch.
2. The drain.
3. Whether the account is stopped.
4. The agent: missing, no disclosure, disclosure not said, not ready, or inbound-only.
5. That the truthful-answer rule was verified on the engine.
6. The account's paperwork and money (`account_dial_blockers`): the agreements, KYC and the D-692 no-cold-calls pledge, credit and the spend cap.
7. The trial line limit.
8. Calling hours. Our window is 09:00 to 21:00 IST, from `calevate_shared/calling_window`, half-open.
9. The person: India-only destination, do-not-call, and consent or expired consent.
10. The declared calling numbers (D-941).

The full lines limit is applied later, inside `agents.service.dispatch_call` (`_hold_engine_line` and `tenancy/call_lines`), as a `lines_full` refusal.

**The single outbound entry point: `agents.service.dispatch_call`**
- It writes the intent row before the engine dials.
- `on_reserved` runs inside the intent transaction.
- It passes `CallContext` with:
  - `context_note`, which becomes THIS CALL's `call_reason`, through `REASON_LINE` in `calevate_shared/engine.py`;
  - `contact_details`, which becomes `call_details` through `DETAILS_LINE`.
- Both are cut to 150 characters (`CALL_VARIABLE_VALUE_MAX`).
- With no `contact_details`, the lead's stored details are used (`campaigns/contact_details.lead_details`).

**Calling back later: `crm/swipe.schedule_lead_callback`**
- Route: `POST /v1/leads/{lead_id}/callbacks`.
- It books one `scheduled_callbacks` row with the `client-schedule:` prefix. The existing dispatch tick dials it through the same gate.
- `check_schedule_time` requires 09:00 to 21:00 IST, at least 2 minutes ahead and at most 30 days ahead.
- `standing_refusal` refuses at once anything that waiting cannot lift: the person, a trial account, or an inbound-only or missing agent.

**The scheduled call-backs themselves: `apps/api/callbacks`**
- These are the scheduled rows, the `GET /v1/callbacks` list and the Call-backs screen.
- `GRACE` is 2 hours.

**Trial and practice calls**
- These are `POST /v1/trial/calls` and `agents/sections/tryIt.tsx`, a separate path for the owner's own phone (D-697 and D-859).
- They are left as they are: they are test calls, not calls to the public.

**Live status: `GET /v1/calls/live`**
- Handled by `crm/live_calls.py`, read on the web by `lib/api/liveCalls.ts`.
- It returns the status, whether the call is live, and when it started, for up to 20 call ids.

**The client sidebar**
- The navigation is listed in `apps/web/src/lib/clientNav.ts` and drawn by `app/c/[slug]/ClientSidebar.tsx`.
- `apps/api/copilot/screens.py` lists the same screens, and `screens_test.py` checks that the two agree.

**Existing quick-dial screens, now redirected**
- On Leads, a row's "Call with AI" button and the swipe or ⋯ "Call now" opened a confirmation dialog that dialled.
- "Call back later" opened its own booking dialog (`components/calls/followUpActions.tsx`).
- On a call's own screen, "Call back now" dialled from the card (`calls/[callId]/CallBackAction.tsx`).

**Pip**
- `call_place` worked by lead id only. It planned with `check_dispatch` and executed through `place_lead_call`.

**Gaps before this work**
- There was no way to call a number that was not already a lead.
- There was no screen for one-off calls.
- Nothing recorded which calls a person had placed.
- The calls API returned only the engine handle, so a screen could not follow the call it had just placed.

## 2. Research (read 5 Nov 2026; sources at the end)

**How other products place one call**
- Bland's API `POST /v1/calls` needs a `phone_number` (E.164) and a `task` or agent/pathway.
  - Context goes in `request_data` variables.
  - `start_time` schedules the call at least 5 minutes ahead, and it can be stopped.
  - Bland's dashboard is reported to have a "Send Call" item in its sidebar. This was seen in a third-party guide only.
- Vapi's call takes `assistantId`, `phoneNumberId` and `customer.number`.
  - Context goes in `assistantOverrides.variableValues`.
  - `schedulePlan.earliestAt` schedules a later call.
  - Statuses are scheduled, queued, ringing, in-progress and ended, plus an `endedReason`: busy, no answer, voicemail.
  - Its dashboard panel has a "Single Number" mode with "Call" and "Schedule Call" buttons.
- Retell needs `from_number` and `to_number`, with `retell_llm_dynamic_variables`. Statuses are registered, ongoing and ended.
- ElevenLabs needs `agent_id`, `agent_phone_number_id`, `to_number` and dynamic variables.
- Exotel's connect-call returns the statuses queued, in-progress, completed, failed, busy and no-answer.

**What this means for our form**
- Every product asks for three things: a number, an agent, and the reason as a per-call variable.
- Scheduling is optional and offered as a time.
- Status is shown as a short progression from ringing to talking to ended, with the ending named.
- Our form asks the same things.
- It uses our existing per-call variables: the reason as `call_reason` and the details as `call_details`.
- It uses our existing call-back booking for "later".

**India rules**

None of these was read on a trai.gov.in primary source. Those pages refused the connection, were scanned images, or returned 404. So every item here is SECONDARY or UNKNOWN, and none is relied on as a new rule.

- **Categories and consent.** SECONDARY: unofficial text of the TCCCPR Second Amendment 2025 on legitquest; Khaitan & Co.
  - A *service* voice call goes to the sender's own customer about their product or service, and needs no explicit consent.
  - A *transactional* call answers a transaction the customer started, within 30 minutes.
  - Any promotional content makes the whole call promotional.
  - Transactional and service calls are outside the definition of UCC. DND "fully block" does not block them under inferred consent.
  - Whether a mere *enquirer* counts as a "customer" is UNKNOWN.
- **Auto-dialler and robocall notice.** SECONDARY: senders must notify their originating operator in advance. Whether an AI agent counts is our inference, UNKNOWN.
- **Calling hours.** UNKNOWN. Exotel's FAQ says promotional calls are limited to 9 am to 9 pm. Another secondary source says 10 am to 9 pm. The primary schedule was not readable.
  - The product keeps its existing window (09:00 to 21:00 IST, `calevate_shared/calling_window`) for every call a person places. That window is a product rule, not a cited TRAI rule. It is not changed here.
- **Number series.** SECONDARY: SCC Online's report of TRAI's 10 Jul 2026 note.
  - 140xx is for promotional calls.
  - 1600xx is for regulated finance and government service and transactional calls.
  - Nothing found requires 1600 for an ordinary small business.
- **Our own rule (D-692).** Outbound needs a verified KYC record and the accepted no-cold-calls pledge. The gate enforces both, and this feature does not touch them.
- **What this means for the design.** A cited rule beats a design wish, and nothing cited here changes the gate. So the form adds no bypass, no new rule and no hours of its own. Every refusal is the gate's own sentence. The screen says plainly that do-not-call, calling hours and credit are checked first.

**The section's name: "Make a call"**
- The products use "Send Call" (Bland), "Call" with "Schedule Call" (Vapi), and "Dialer" (Retell, for campaign-style calling).
- "Dialer" reads as campaigns in this trade, and in our own Campaigns screen aliases.
- "Send call" is not how an Indian shop owner says it.
- "Make a call" is a verb phrase in plain English (ux-writing: start with the verb, match the user's intent). It sits beside Campaigns, which handles calls to many people.

## 3. What was built

**Backend**
- `apps/api/crm/one_call.py` adds `POST /v1/one-calls` (`leads:dispatch`, `Idempotency-Key` required). The person is named by exactly one of:
  - `phone`: find or make the lead on (tenant, phone, agent), the key the call's own filing upserts on;
  - `lead_id`;
  - `call_id`: follow up that call through `place_call_back`.
- The request also carries `agent_id`, `reason` (required for a person; at most 150 characters), optional `details` (at most 150) and optional `at`.
- **A new number is checked by the gate before any lead is made.**
  - A refused number (do-not-call, non-India, an agent that cannot dial) leaves no lead behind.
  - An allowed one gets its lead committed in its own transaction before the dial. That way a retry of the same attempt finds the same lead and is answered from the claim.
- **Now** goes through `lead_dial.place_lead_call`. **At a time** goes through `swipe.schedule_lead_callback`, with the reason and details folded into the booking note (at most 200 characters).
- Outside the calling hours, the refusal carries `opens_at` and says "You can call from …".
- `lead_dial` changes:
  - `place_lead_call` takes `details`, which goes into THIS CALL's details line under "Note", ahead of the lead's stored details.
  - Both dial functions mark the intent row `calls.placed_by_person` inside the intent transaction, and return `call_id`.
  - `calling_hours_reason` takes a verb.
- Migration `d3a9f5c1e7b4` (after `c7e1a4d9b2f6`) adds `calls.placed_by_person boolean NOT NULL DEFAULT false` and a partial index. It is an existing table with its existing RLS, and it is reversible.
- Reads, both of which keep one way of listing:
  - `GET /v1/calls?placed_by_person=true` lists the recent one-off calls.
  - `GET /v1/callbacks?booked_by_person=true` lists the calls booked for later.
- Pip's `call_place` now takes `lead_id` or `phone` (plus an optional `name`). It plans with the gate as before and executes through `place_one_call`. There is still one action and one path.

**Web**
- A new sidebar entry, **Make a call**, sits between Needs attention and Campaigns. It leads to the route `/c/{slug}/make-a-call`.
  - The page shows a whole-screen refusal unless the person holds `leads:dispatch`.
- **The form**
  - Number (with a +91 prefix shown) and an optional name.
  - The agent: only agents that can dial (`canDialOut`), with the first one or `?agent=` preselected.
  - "Why are you calling?". Its example comes from the business's own generated `outbound_purpose`, and no example is shown until that exists.
  - Optional details.
  - A Now / At a time switch, using the existing day and slot picker.
  - The button reads **Call now** or **Book the call**.
- **Refusals** show the gate's own sentence. Two extras:
  - "Book it for …" when the refusal is about calling hours.
  - "Add credit" or "See your limit" for the money rules.
  - Field errors from the server show under the field.
- **After Call now**, a live card replaces the form.
  - It shows Ringing, then Talking, then Ended, from `GET /v1/calls/live`, and keeps its last answer after the read stops.
  - Its links are "See the call" (which becomes "Open the call" once it ends) and "Make another call".
- **After Book the call**, the screen says "Booked for …" and offers "Call it off" (the undo) and "Make another call".
- **Below the form** are "Booked for later" and "Recent calls you made", each with loading, error and empty states.
- **Every other Call button now opens this form.** The quick-dial dialogs and the per-door hooks were removed: `useCallLead`, `useCallBack` and `useScheduleCallback`, and `CallDialog` and `LaterDialog` in `followUpActions.tsx`.
  - Leads row "Call with AI" opens `?lead=&agent=`.
  - The swipe or ⋯ "Call now" or "Call back" does the same.
  - "Call back later" opens `?lead=&agent=&when=later`.
  - The call screen's "Call back now" opens `?call=`.
  - Links carry ids only, never a phone number.
