# D3: the call itself, end to end (deep audit, 10 Nov 2026)

Audit D3, redis db 3. Base `f45a32f8f` (fast-forwarded and confirmed). Area: agent config,
then `compose_engine_prompt` and the call-script compile, then the ThinnestAI agent body and
per-call fields, then Studio's Cartesia settings, then in-call lookups, then post-call
mistake checks, plus the script lint and turn checks against the real bad calls.

Trigger: the founder's call to Varsha (Rytu Khata, live Studio agent, outbound te-IN) at
00:58 IST on 10 Nov 2026. Speed 0.90, emotion neutral, caller memory on, so
`pastConversations` is `recap`. What happened, as the founder described it:

- the agent opened with the business name only;
- it asked whether to continue a remembered install conversation;
- after "continue" it said "we have installed the app", though nothing on this call said so;
- the speech sounded very bad.

No transcript or recording of that call was available to this audit. The causes below are the
ones the code and the vendor's own pages prove. Anything that needs the call's own rows is
marked UNVERIFIED, and the query that settles it is under PRODUCTION CHECK.

## Findings

### D3-01 (P1, product behaviour; FOUNDER DECISION, not changed): `recap` on a call the agent places replaces the call's purpose with the last call's, and the last call's outcome reads as true now

This is what produced the 00:58 call's second and third symptoms.

- **The code.** `apps/api/engine/thinnest.py:2373` sends `"pastConversations": "recap" if
  cfg.caller_memory_enabled else "fresh"`. It does the same for every direction, and for
  every call the agent places, owner test calls included.
- **The vendor's documented behaviour** (`channels/voice.md:379-382`, 29 Oct snapshot): recap
  "opens by saying you have spoken before and what it was about, then asks whether they would
  like to carry on or talk about something else. On a call you placed, it answers what they
  say first and recaps right after."
  - That is the sequence the founder heard: the opening, then "shall we continue the install?"
- **What the model is given** (ThinnestAI's email of 28 Oct 2026, VENDOR-STATED, §39 item 7):
  "The same number's earlier calls are part of the call's own conversation, so the agent
  still uses them".
  - The founder's number has called Varsha many times since 5 Nov. On 5 Nov the outcome was
    "App installed successfully" (memory 0393ef65).
  - So after "continue", "we have installed the app" is the model continuing that earlier
    conversation. It is not an invention out of nothing.
- **Why our rules do not stop it.**
  - `CALL_REASON_RULE` (`engine.py:4740`) forbids "have or use something unless they or
    THIS CALL said so". In the model's history, *they said so*, on an earlier call.
  - `ENGINE_MEMORY_RULE` (`engine.py:3689`) only says "speak of an earlier conversation only
    when what you were given ... shows one", and the history does show one.
  - `OPENING_CONTINUES_RULE` says a hello or yes means "go on with why you call". The
    vendor's recap instruction asks about carrying on the old topic instead. The two
    instructions compete, and on this call the vendor's won.
- **There is no per-call switch.** `CallOverrides` takes only `voice`, `language`,
  `maxCallSeconds`, `ringSeconds` and `callInstructions` (`place-call.md:1254-1290`).
  `pastConversations` is per agent.
- **What changed in code:** only the comment above `ENGINE_MEMORY_RULE` (`engine.py:3680`).
  It cited "opens by recapping" from create-agent and missed both facts above, so the next
  reader could not see why the rule does not cover this. The rule sentence is unchanged.
- **Founder wording:** see FOUNDER DECISIONS 1 and 2.

### D3-02 (P2, evidence gap; vendor question corrected): §51 asked about a te-IN agent, but every agent is `auto` on the wire, and our "Hear it" sample is the one request that names the language

- **The live call.** `thinnest.py:2517` sends `language: "auto"`, and the voice block sends
  `voice.language: null` (`_AUTO_LANGUAGE`, founder rule of 19 and 28 Oct 2026). On auto the
  recogniser "has to decide the language afresh for every sentence"
  (`channels/voice.md:563-567`).
- **The sample.** `preview_own_key_voice` (`thinnest.py:4026`) sends `language: te-IN` (the
  client and admin preview routes pass the agent's `language_primary`).
- So the founder can approve a speed or emotion on a sample spoken under te-IN, and then hear
  a call spoken under whatever ThinnestAI hands Cartesia for an auto agent.
- The drafted §51 question 2 asked "for an agent whose language is te-IN", which is a
  configuration we never send. Its answer would not have described our calls.
- **Fixed in the draft** (`docs/vendor/thinnest-replies-2026-10-12.md` §51):
  - a new bullet states the auto fact and the sample divergence;
  - question 2 now asks about an auto agent on a Telugu call;
  - new question 5 asks whether `emotion` reaches Cartesia on a Telugu call at all, and
    whether `speed` reaches Cartesia unchanged.
- Not proposed: pinning the language. That is the founder's standing rule, "never propose
  pinning or restricting the language again" (memory 3e8f727b).
- UNVERIFIED until ThinnestAI answers: what Cartesia is actually sent for an auto agent.

### D3-03 (P3, no defect): what speed and emotion mean on the wire for Studio

The Studio settings arrive exactly as stored:

- `voice.speakingSpeed` is null on a Studio agent (`_speaking_speed_wire`, `thinnest.py:1849`);
- the byok-voice PUT carries `speed: 0.9`, `emotion: "neutral"`, and the model D-977 pinned;
- each is read back, with a refusal on any mismatch (`_apply_own_key_voice`).

What they do in Cartesia:

- **Speed.** Cartesia says "Sonic treats speed as guidance, not a strict multiplier". It
  suggests `0.7` rather than `0.9` when you want speech noticeably slower
  (docs.cartesia.ai/build-with-cartesia/sonic-3/volume-speed-emotion, read 10 Nov 2026). So
  0.90 is a small nudge, not "10% slower".
- **Emotion.** The same page still says "Emotion tags are supported only for English", and so
  does `set-agent-byok-voice.md:9` ("English only").
  - The founder's 21 Oct playground screenshot showed emotion offered for Telugu (memory
    eb0abec1). It is not proof that emotion changes Telugu audio.
  - Whether `neutral` does anything on a Telugu call is UNVERIFIED (§51 question 5).
- **Telugu support.** Sonic 3.6 lists `te` among its 44 languages, and
  `sonic-3.6-2026-08-27` is a stable dated snapshot
  (docs.cartesia.ai/build-with-cartesia/tts-models/latest, read 10 Nov 2026). D-977's pin is
  a supported model for Telugu.

No code-level cause of "sounds very bad" is proven. The candidates are:

- what language or normalization ThinnestAI sends Cartesia for an auto agent (D3-02);
- prosody seams between the separately spoken pieces of one reply (§51 question 1);
- the reply text itself.

None of the three can be settled from this tree.

### D3-04 (P3, coverage statement): no check could have caught the 10 Nov call; the 9 Nov ones are covered

- **Script lint.** `script_lint.order_problems` (the 9 Nov fix) reads the owner's script. The
  10 Nov claim came from the engine's memory of earlier calls, which no script holds.
- **Practice runs.** These run the agent without engine memory (`agents/test_conversations`),
  so a practice run never recaps.
- **Real-call mistakes.** `call_mistakes.mistakes_in` does not judge `presumed_state`, which
  needs the script's lines (`turn_checks.SCRIPT_KINDS`). `false_history` treats any earlier
  lead as prior contact (`pipeline._prior_contact`). Here the earlier calls really happened,
  so judging true history as false would be wrong.
- **The 9 Nov patterns are pinned by tests:**
  - "you already use the app", the double introduction, the spelled web address, Latin
    words, and the same opener: `tests/presupposition_2026_11_09_test.py` and
    `tests/rytu_khata_call_2026_11_09_test.py`.
- **A lint gap I examined and did not change.** `_presumes_use` covers only *use*, not
  *installed* or *has*. A step that takes the install as given and is reached without asking
  would pass. I did not widen it:
  - in an install-guide script, every step after the setup step legitimately presumes the
    install;
  - the 10 Nov claim did not come from a script step, so widening the check would be a change
    no real call justifies ("dont do meaningless changes").
  - Recorded here so the next real call that shows it has the analysis ready.

### D3-05 (UNVERIFIED): "opened with the business name only"

- For an outbound agent whose AI notice introduces it, `join_first_words` (`engine.py:4463`)
  drops every sentence before the opening's last that names the agent (`_without_introduction`,
  D-965). The person's name and title carry over (9 Nov).
- Whether that left Varsha's first turn with only the business name depends on two things:
  - Varsha's live opening line;
  - whether the woven notice opening (D-971) already said who is calling.
- Neither is in this tree. The first query under PRODUCTION CHECK prints the greeting the
  engine holds, so the founder can see the exact first words.
- This is notice-adjacent code (`join_first_words`), which the brief puts off limits, so
  nothing was changed even speculatively.

## FOUNDER DECISIONS

1. **Recap on calls the agent places.**
   - Today: every agent with caller memory on gets `pastConversations: "recap"`, and that
     includes every outbound call and every owner test call. The vendor then recaps the last
     call and asks whether to carry on, after the person's first words.
   - The evidence is D3-01. Pick one:
     - (a) Keep recap everywhere and change the two rule sentences (decision 2).
     - (b) Send `fresh` for agents whose direction is `outbound`. They then never recap or
       use earlier calls. Inbound and "both" agents keep recap, because a "both" agent is one
       agent on the vendor side and cannot be split per call. One line in
       `engine/thinnest._agent_body` plus its test. The drift sweep already compares
       `voice.pastConversations`.
     - (c) Keep recap and accept it.
   - `quiet` stays rejected (8 Oct 2026).
2. **Rule sentences, if recap stays on placed calls** (a or c). Exact proposed text:
   - `CALL_REASON_RULE`, current: `Never say they enquired, showed interest, have or use
     something unless they or THIS CALL said so; a step's "sounds like" is only an example.
     Asked what we hold? Say THIS CALL's name, details.`
     Proposed: `Never say they enquired, showed interest, have or use something unless they
     said so on this call or THIS CALL says so; a step's "sounds like" is only an example.
     Asked what we hold? Say THIS CALL's name, details.`
     The change: "they ... said so" becomes "said so on this call". The earlier calls are in
     the model's history (§39 item 7), so "they said so" is met by a call days ago.
   - `ENGINE_MEMORY_RULE`, current: `Speak of an earlier conversation only when what you
     were given about this caller shows one; never invent one.`
     Proposed: `Speak of an earlier conversation only when what you were given about this
     caller shows one; never invent one. It tells you what was talked about then, not what
     is true now: ask before saying they did, have or use anything. On a call you place,
     why you call comes first.`
   - Cost: both change `platform_prompt_revision`, so the live apply republishes live agents.
     `ENGINE_MEMORY_RULE` adds about 150 characters to agents with memory on. The prompt
     budget test (`tests/agent_prompt_budget_test.py`) has to be re-read for headroom.
3. **Emotion on Telugu Studio agents.**
   - Cartesia's page and ThinnestAI's page both say emotion is English-only. Varsha runs on
     `neutral`.
   - Until §51 question 5 is answered, an ear test on a real call (not "Hear it", see D3-02)
     with the emotion cleared tells whether it matters.
   - That is an owner setting on the agent; no code change.
4. **Send §51** (now corrected). It is the only route to what Cartesia is actually given on
   our calls.
