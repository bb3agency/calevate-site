## Fixed

### Z2-01 (P1, compliance): a halt or a suppression made while a dial was in flight never reached it (X5-N1 / D-428 `local:` window)

- **Where.** `apps/api/agents/service.py::dispatch_call`. Both recalls skip placeholder rows:
  `queued_dial_scan` filters `engine_call_id NOT LIKE 'local:%'`
  (`alembic/versions/b7e4c0a63f29_recall_reaches_ringing_dials.py:66`), and the DNC recall
  uses the same scan. A dial is a placeholder from its intent row until `_confirm_dial`,
  which spans the pre-dial caller lookup and the `POST /calls` round trip.
- **Failure.** The big red switch is thrown, or the person says "stop calling me" on another
  call (`add_to_dnc` enqueues its recall), during that window. The recall job scans once,
  sees a `local:` row it may not touch, and finishes. The handle is stamped a moment later
  and nothing scans again, so the phone rings after the halt or after the DNC entry. The
  campaign tick narrows the halt window by re-reading the switch per contact, but cannot
  close it; the DNC half was not narrowed at all. Applies to every dial path (campaign,
  call-backs, the lead button, Make a call, test calls), because they all end in
  `dispatch_call`.
- **Fix.** After `_confirm_dial` commits the handle, `_recall_if_stopped_while_dialling`
  reads the halt row (`ops.service.read_halt_state`, durable, on the same connection) and the
  person-level refusal (`compliance.service.person_level_refusal`). Halted: it enqueues the
  existing halt recall (`ops.halt.queue_dial_recall`). DNC or withdrawn consent: it enqueues
  the existing DNC recall for that number (`compliance.dnc_recall.enqueue_dnc_recall`, cause
  `consent` for a withdrawal). No new recall logic: the existing jobs re-scan and now find
  the stamped dial; a dial they already stopped is skipped by `recall_requested_at`. The
  race is closed in both orders: a halt/entry committed before the read is seen here; one
  committed after it enqueues its own recall, whose scan runs after the handle is stamped.
  Never raises (the vendor has accepted the call); a failure is alarmed as
  `dial_bookkeeping_failed` with step `stop_recheck`.
- **Tests** (both fail on the pre-fix source, run against `HEAD`'s `service.py`):
  - `test_a_number_suppressed_while_its_dial_was_in_flight_is_recalled`: the DNC entry lands
    inside the engine call; afterwards a recall is queued for the number and the DNC recall
    job stops the dial's handle.
  - `test_a_halt_thrown_while_a_dial_was_in_flight_recalls_it`: the halt lands inside the
    engine call; afterwards `recall_queued_dials` is enqueued.
  - `test_an_ordinary_dial_asks_for_no_recall`: guard against over-recalling.

### Z2-02 (P2, compliance): a number written `+91 (0) 98765 43210` was stored as a different, undialable number

- **Where.** `apps/api/ingest/service.py::normalize_phone`, the one normaliser for campaign
  CSV uploads (`campaigns.service.add_contacts`), CRM leads added to a campaign, the DNC
  page and bulk import (`compliance/dnc.py`), Make a call (`crm/one_call.normalised_phone`)
  and lead webhooks.
- **Failure.** The `+` branch kept every digit, so `+91 (0) 98765 43210` (country code plus
  trunk prefix, a common printed form) became `+9109876543210`. It passes the E.164 shape
  check, so it was accepted, not counted malformed. As a campaign contact it rings nobody.
  As a do-not-call entry it never equals the person's real `+919876543210`, so the client
  reads "1 added" and the person stays dialable. That is the D-562 defect in another
  spelling.
- **Fix.** Thirteen digits `910` + a mobile shape (6-9) fold to `+91` + the ten digits,
  with or without the `+`. Nothing else is loosened: without a `+`, `91 0` before a
  non-mobile shape is still unreadable (with a `+`, any 10-15 digits were already taken
  as written, unchanged here).
- **Tests:** `test_a_number_written_with_country_code_and_trunk_prefix_is_the_same_number`,
  `test_a_suppression_typed_with_the_trunk_prefix_stops_the_real_number`. Both fail on the
  pre-fix source.

## Checked, no defect found (read at source)

- `compliance.service.check_dispatch`: halt read fresh (or by the tick just before), drain,
  stopped account, agent and AI line and first words, truthful-answer drift, KYC/pledge/
  agreements/spend cap/dispute/platform fee/credits, trial line cap, calling hours
  half-open in IST, person level (India only, erased, DNC tenant+global live, consent), A2P
  declaration. Every dial path calls it before `dispatch_call`.
- `workers/campaign_dispatch._dispatch_for_campaign`: claim commits before the first dial,
  each dial its own transaction; per contact it re-reads the campaign's status and narrowed
  window (`campaign_dialable_now`), the halt past the memo, and the gate. Person-level
  refusals settle the contact; everything else refunds the attempt. `lines_full` stops the
  rest of the batch. Unconfirmed dials end the contact (no second ring).
- Retry ladder (`_record_failure`): DB clock, `attempts` already incremented at claim, rung
  index right; `_settle_unanswered_dials` waits 2 min (busy/no answer) and 20 min (failed).
- Campaign completion (`_settle_finished_campaign`, `complete_or_rearm`): only off
  `running`, counts `pending`+`dialing`, repeats re-arm to `scheduled` without re-dialling
  reached people.
- Launch (`launch_campaign`): agent row lock, gate, DNC scrub, CAS from draft/scheduled
  with `archived_at IS NULL`. Resume (`assert_resumable`): archived, complaint review,
  credit, spend cap. Credit auto-resume skips archived and archived-agent campaigns.
- Campaign window validation narrows only, HH:MM, half-open.
- `normalize_phone` handles Telugu/Devanagari digits, `0` trunk prefix, `91`+ten, refuses
  `++`, foreign shapes without a `+`.
- `crm/one_call.place_one_call`: gate before a new lead is written; booked calls ask only
  standing refusals now and the gate at fire time.
