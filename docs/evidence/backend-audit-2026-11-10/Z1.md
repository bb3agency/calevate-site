## Fixed

### Z1-01 (P2): the over-limit refusal on auto top-up told the owner to do something the screen does not offer

`apps/api/billing/auto_recharge.py` `save_auto_recharge`, the `auto_recharge_over_mandate`
refusal (was line 362).

How it fails: the owner approves a payment method while "Top up by" reads ₹2,000. The approval's
per-payment limit is that amount (`AutoRechargePanel.approve` sends `max_debit_inr: amount`, and
`begin_mandate` stores it as `mandate_max_inr`). Later the owner raises "Top up by" to ₹3,000 and
switches auto top-up on. The switch is refused with "Lower the recharge amount, or approve a new
payment method." But beside a confirmed approval the panel shows only "Withdraw approval". The
approve control appears only when the method is not confirmed, so the owner could not find the
action they were told to take.

Fix: the remediation now names the steps the screen actually supports: "Lower the recharge amount
to ₹2,000 or less. For a higher amount, withdraw the approval, then approve the payment method
again with the new amount." After a withdrawal (`cancel_mandate` sets the status to `cancelled`),
the panel shows the approve control again, and that control sends the amount currently typed.

Test: `tests/auto_recharge_flow_test.py::test_switching_on_above_the_mandate_limit_is_refused_and_within_it_resets_failures`
now asserts the remediation. It failed on the old code
(`'withdraw the approval' in 'Lower the recharge amount, or approve a new payment method.'`).

## Checked at source, no defect found

- Auto-recharge charge path (`maybe_start_recharge`, `settle_charge`, `_fail_charge`,
  `expire_unanswered`, `charge_verdict`): the settings row is locked, the one pending charge is
  enforced by a partial unique index, and the monthly cap counts pending and captured charges.
  A claim whose order creation never completed still holds a `claim:` placeholder order id. It
  is failed with no provider read, which is safe because no recurring payment is created before
  the real order id is committed. X1-03's late-authorisation handling is intact and was not
  redone.
- `payment_events.apply_captured`, `_apply_fee_payment`, `apply_refund_processed`,
  `apply_refund_failed`, `apply_dispute`: each takes the inbox claim and writes the ledger in
  one transaction. Refs are taken under `lock_tenant_credits`. A platform-fee payment never
  reaches the wallet.
- `reconciliation.reconcile`: a platform-fee payment that is already recorded comes back as a
  duplicate, so no second credit. A refund with no top-up on the wallet is listed as
  unexplained and not debited.
- `payments.claim_refund` / `unspent_credit_of_payment` / `_claw_back_pack_bonus`: every ceiling
  is checked under the lock against committed claims, and the cumulative bonus target is clamped.
- `first_payment.on_payment_credited`: the ₹1.00 mandate authorisation
  (`MANDATE_AUTH_AMOUNT_INR`) is below `MIN_TOPUP_INR`. It therefore does not end a trial or
  mark the account paid.
- `credit_expiry.expire_lot`, `credit_routes` adjustment (cumulative reversible ceiling, step-up
  on the debit direction only, content-addressed ref whose collapse cost is documented),
  `number_rental.charge_number_rental` (period key per IST month, clamped anchors),
  `invoice.build_invoice` (bill of supply while unregistered: ₹0 tax, total = subtotal, D-659).

## Not fixed

- **UNVERIFIED (P2 if true): a signed `refund.processed` without our tenant note is refused with
  4xx, and Razorpay keeps retrying it.** `payments.extract_refund` raises
  `payment_tenant_unresolved` when `notes.calevate_tenant_id` is missing. The receiver re-raises
  it after alarming `razorpay_money_unapplied`. Razorpay says it retries "in exponential backoff
  policy for 24 hours" and "If the webhooks continue to fail for 24 hours, the webhook is
  disabled" (`https://razorpay.com/docs/build/llm-docs/webhooks/best-practices.md`, read 10 Nov
  2026). Two kinds of refund would carry no note from us: one made from the dashboard, and the
  refund Razorpay itself makes of an uncaptured payment or a late payment on a paid order
  (`payments/payments/late-authorisation.md`, read 10 Nov 2026: "the amount is automatically
  refunded to the customer"). Two things are not documented on the pages I read
  (`webhooks/refunds.md`, `payments/refunds/subscribe-to-webhooks.md`, `late-authorisation.md`,
  all 10 Nov 2026): whether those automatic refunds emit `refund.processed`, and whether one
  failing event (while other events succeed) counts as "the webhook continues to fail". Proving
  it needs a test-mode rehearsal (OPERATIONS gate 44H). If it is real, the fix is to ack such an
  event as unexplained instead of returning 4xx. The daily reconciliation already lists it.
- **P3, not fixed: auto top-up switched off with the wrong reason when the approving member's
  user row is deleted.** `maybe_start_recharge` refuses with `auto_recharge_needs_contact`
  ("Approve the payment method again") when `authorised_by` is NULL. That happens only through
  `ON DELETE SET NULL` on `users`, so only after a user is erased. Each 5-minute sweep then
  claims a charge and fails it, three times (`auto_recharge_max_failures`). Each failure sends a
  "recharge failed" email. Auto top-up then goes off with "the last automatic payments did not
  go through", although no payment was tried. Left alone: it is a narrow edge case, and the right
  fix (check the payer before claiming and say why) touches the notice copy.
- Refunding credit that already lapsed. When a refund names a lot that expired,
  `remove_credit_from_lots` first restates the lapsed credit and then takes the refund off. So a
  refund can return money for credit the expiry took. That is an operator's step-up decision,
  not a defect in the code. Whether lapsed credit should be refundable at all is for the founder
  to decide (D-840 / Refund Policy).
