# Backend audit 10 Nov 2026: lane Z4 (after the call)

Base: `565a03ff2`. Branch `worktree-agent-a4b65a39b229355a9`. No migration. Tests in

## Fixed

### Z4-01 (P2): "See missed calls" could not show the set it counted
- **Where.** `apps/api/crm/lens.py:call_where` filtered on one `status`. The home count
  (`apps/web/src/app/c/[slug]/NextSteps.tsx`, `today.failed + today.no_answer`) is the dashboard's
  `no_answer` class (no_answer, busy, voicemail) plus `failed` (`crm/service.DAILY_CALL_CLASSES`).
  It also bucketed calls by `started_at` (`_DAILY_7D_SQL`), while the log filters by
  `COALESCE(started_at, created_at)` (`lens.CALL_AT`).
- **Failure.** Two failures. First, if a day had both failed and unanswered calls, the link
  opened the whole day, completed calls included. Second, a dial that ended without ringing
  (`failed`, `started_at` NULL) was listed under today but was not counted.
- **Fix.** `lens.CALL_STATUS_GROUPS["missed"]`, which `call_where` turns into
  `c.status = ANY(...)`. The list, the CSV export, Pip's `calls_find` and saved views all read
  it through the lens. The home link is now `/calls?status=missed&day=…&test_calls=false`, and
  the Calls screen accepts `missed` ("Only missed calls" chip). The daily chart files a call
  under the day of `CALL_AT`. A call still queued with no `started_at` stays out, so
  `test_a_call_that_was_never_dialled_is_in_no_bucket` holds. The bound is an OR over
  `ix_calls_tenant_started` and `ix_calls_tenant_created`. I regenerated `openapi.json` with
  `scripts.check_openapi_fresh --write` and `schema.d.ts` with openapi-typescript. Only
  descriptions changed on the wire. I updated the web test `dashboard.test.tsx`.
- **Commit.** `b4ea18e95`.
- **Tests.** `test_the_missed_group_is_the_dashboards_missed_count` and
  `test_the_missed_link_lists_exactly_the_calls_the_home_screen_counted` (count 5 = list 5,
  including the unstarted failed dial; trial and paid test calls are left out of both).

### Z4-02 (P2): "no test calls" kept a paying account's test calls
- **Where.** `crm/lens.py` left out only `c.trial_call` in two places: the calls clause and the
  leads clause. The dashboard (`lead_definition.REAL_CALL_SQL`), analytics and performance leave
  out `trial_call OR paid_test_call` (D-859).
- **Failure.** With test calls hidden, the Calls log, its export, the Leads table and any
  bulk action built on the lens still held the owner's own test calls. One such action is
  Pip's "Call them again" plan (`test_calls` in its lens). So "missed calls today" and its link
  disagreed by every paid test call.
- **Fix.** One `_TEST_CALL` predicate used by both clauses.
- **Commit.** `b4ea18e95`.
- **Tests.** `test_no_test_calls_leaves_out_a_paying_accounts_test_calls` and
  `test_no_test_calls_leaves_out_leads_whose_latest_call_was_a_paid_test`.

### Z4-03 (P2): an after-call call back on a hidden-caller call rang the business itself
- **Where.** `apps/workers/pipeline.py` STEP 3d' passed the network's number to
  `after_call_callbacks.book_requested_callback`. On a forwarded call that showed the
  business's own number (D-746, `calls.caller_id_hidden`, set by STEP 1e), other paths already
  handle this. The lead (`_upsert_lead`) uses the call-back number the caller gave. Opt-out
  (`_maybe_record_opt_out`) stands down. The in-call tool (`worker/engine_actions`) knows the
  caller is hidden. The after-call booking had no such check.
- **Failure.** A caller says "call me later" on a forwarded call. The after-call call back is
  booked to the business's own number, so the dispatcher rings and bills the business, and the
  caller who asked is never called.
- **Fix.** `_number_to_ring` reads `caller_id_hidden` and `forwarding_test_id`. A hidden caller
  is rung on the number they gave (`other_number`), or no call back is booked. Our own
  forwarding test books none. `callback_number` moved from `pipeline._callback_number` to
  `after_call_callbacks`, so the lead and the call back read one helper.
- **Commit.** `83f638525`.
- **Tests.** `test_a_hidden_caller_is_rung_on_the_number_they_gave_not_the_business` and
  `test_a_hidden_caller_who_gave_no_number_gets_no_call_back`. Before the fix: the booking went
  to `+919900011122`, the business, and a call back was booked with no number given.

## Checked, no defect found (read at source)
- **Lead dedupe after erasure.** `workers/retention.py:3041` anonymises `phone_e164` on erasure.
  A later call from the same person creates a fresh lead rather than updating the hidden,
  erased one.
- **Lead re-run counting.** `_upsert_lead`'s `call` timeline event is the filed-once marker,
  and `last_call_id` and `first_call_id` move in call time. The lead stays within
  (tenant, phone, agent).
- **Call back now.** `crm/service.plan_callback` dials the lead's number. For a hidden caller
  that is the given number, not the network's.
- **Callback dispatch.** `workers/callbacks.dispatch_due_callbacks` commits the claim before
  the dial and gives each dial its own transaction. Unconfirmed dials are settled and never
  retried.
- **Extraction metering.** `_meter_extraction` meters on a fresh ref per paid pass under
  `ASSIST_FEATURE_CALL_EXTRACTION`. This is our cost; the client's allowance never counts it.
  A re-run that pays the model again is recorded again, which is deliberate.
- **Daily summary.** `notifications/daily_summary.py` leaves out test calls by
  `trial_call OR paid_test_call`.
