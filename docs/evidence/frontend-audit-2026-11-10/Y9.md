- Background: since V1-03 (909dbe75a, the invite sign-out fix) the server serialises two redemptions of one link. The second one (a second tab, or the email opened twice) gets `422 invitation_invalid` and writes nothing. Backend end to end is correct.

- Commit: 243de1440.

## Checked, no defect found
- **`?next=` / open redirect.** `lib/authn/nextPath.safeNextPath` accepts only a same-origin path inside the realm prefix. It refuses absolute URLs, `//`, backslashes, control characters, the other realm and `..` escapes, and caps the length; this is pinned in `tests/authnOtp.test.tsx`.
  - Every consumer goes through it: client and admin sign-in destinations, the Google callback (`done.next`), and the Google button's `next`.
  - The OAuth connect return builds `/c/<slug>/integrations` from its own session record, not from the URL.
- **Redirect loops.** `redirectToClientSignIn` skips the guest paths. `withNext` never nests the sign-in path in itself. Guest pages bounce a signed-in person to the validated destination.
- **Pricing figures.** `/pricing` already follows `headlineVoice` and `voice_not_offered` (W6-03). Every ₹ figure on the public pages comes from `/v1/public/rate-card`. The only literal rupee amounts left are the labelled telecaller salary benchmarks inside the calculator.
- **Internal links.** Every literal `href` on the public and auth pages resolves to a route. Every in-page anchor (`#self-serve`, `#controls`, `#every-dial`, `#where-it-runs`, `#access`, `#testing`, `#documents`, `#glossary`, `#reading`, `#how`) has its target id. All legal slugs exist.

None overflowed.

- **Metadata and canonicals.** Every public page uses `publicPageMetadata`, and its canonical matched its host in the browser (`/signup` points at app., per W6-05). Auth pages carry titles and sit under `/auth/` in robots.
